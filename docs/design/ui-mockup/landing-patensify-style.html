08Questions

The things people ask us.

What exactly does EV Scope do?+
It takes your real routes and vehicle data, simulates every hour of a full weather year, and sizes batteries, chargers and the grid connection to the single hardest week — then exports the documents you need to act on it.
Do I need to prepare clean data first?+
No. Drop in the telematics exports, route plans and datasheets you already have. EV Scope fills the gaps with sensible, source-tagged defaults you can review and override.
Why size to the worst week instead of the average?+
Because an average never has to run. Fleets fail on the coldest, busiest week. Sizing to that week is the only way to guarantee the design works in service.
Who is it for?+
Municipal, refuse, bus and utility fleets — with templates tuned for European and Nordic procurement and grant processes. Consultants running feasibility studies for those fleets, too.
How do I join the pilot?+
Bring one route set or one depot. We'll run the full study with you in an afternoon. Email hello@evscope.dk to start.
