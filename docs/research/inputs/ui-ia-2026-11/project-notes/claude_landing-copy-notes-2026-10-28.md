### One Study, Read Four Ways

*Every run answers the specific questions your stakeholders will actually ask.*

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  [Fleet Owners]  |  [Truck Makers / OEMs]  |  [Municipalities]  |  [Grid & Charge]  |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

```

#### 1. Fleet Owners

* **The Decision:** Know which routes can go electric right now, and what infrastructure your depot actually needs to survive winter.
* **What EV Scope Gives You:**
* Battery sizing built for your **hardest day**, not an optimistic average.
* Exact charger counts and grid capacity requirements before you sign any purchase orders.
* Year-by-year cash flow comparison against diesel.
* A verifiable audit trail your board and bank can check line-by-line.


* **Key Questions Answered:**
* *“Will the trucks actually make it through January and February?”* $\rightarrow$ Sized for the worst weather day with batteries aged to year 8.
* *“How big of a grid connection do we need?”* $\rightarrow$ Peak power in kW and amperes, factoring in local DSO lead times.



#### 2. Truck Makers (OEMs)

* **The Decision:** Prove your vehicle specs on the customer's actual operational routes, bypassing generic test cycles.
* **What EV Scope Gives You:**
* Your exact vehicle spec sheet simulated on the buyer’s real-world routes.
* Evidence-backed answers for strict municipal and private tender requirements.
* Transparent trade-offs between battery capacity, gross weight, and payload.
* Objective comparisons showing how your vehicle matches competing offers on the exact same case.


* **Key Questions Answered:**
* *“Does our 300 kWh pack win this tender?”* $\rightarrow$ Usable kWh verified at year-8 degradation on their exact topography.
* *“What charging power window must we guarantee?”* $\rightarrow$ Per-truck and depot-level power thresholds.



#### 3. Municipalities

* **The Decision:** Write bulletproof tender requirements from real collection routes and plan depot infrastructure that outlives the initial contract.
* **What EV Scope Gives You:**
* Tender specifications derived directly from your own municipal collection routes.
* Clear frameworks for tendering vehicles and charging infrastructure either separately or together.
* Long-term depot and grid planning data.
* Verified CO₂ reduction metrics ready for council reports and national grant applications.


* **Key Questions Answered:**
* *“What minimum battery capacity should we legally require in the tender?”* $\rightarrow$ Derived strictly from worst-case route energy demands.
* *“Who should own the chargers?”* $\rightarrow$ Capital expenditure spread efficiently over the asset's real operational lifetime.



#### 4. Charge Operators & Grid Companies (DSOs)

* **The Decision:** Size depot connections and evaluate grid flexibility using real driving telemetry rather than rough thumb-rules.
* **What EV Scope Gives You:**
* Depot peak load, coincidence factors, and hourly load profiles.
* Full vs. limited grid access comparisons (FNA / BNA evaluations).
* Quantifiable flexibility potential offered by the parked fleet during off-peak hours.
* Grid connection application data structured to match DSO screening requirements.


* **Key Questions Answered:**
* *“How much can the depot load be curtailed during grid stress?”* $\rightarrow$ Explicit what-if cut scenarios guaranteed to finish charging before the 06:00 shift.
* *“Is this depot large enough to qualify for FCR or mFRR ancillary markets?”* $\rightarrow$ Checked directly against Energinet minimum capacity thresholds.

### The Six Standardized Deliverables

*Generated from a single run. Same numbers, formatted precisely for the person who has to sign off.*

* **Feasibility Report** *(For Fleet Owners & Board)*
The complete master study detailing every input, calculation, and underlying source used in the model.
* **Grid Connection Application** *(For Radius, Cerius, and DSOs)*
Structured technical data addressing everything the grid company asks for during initial capacity screening and grid integration review.
* **Tender Annex** *(For Buyers & Bidders)*
Objective, route-based energy and operational requirements that every supplier offer must satisfy.
* **Financial Model** *(For CFO & Finance Teams)*
Transparent year-by-year cash flow analysis tracking total cost of ownership of electric fleets against diesel baselines.
* **Grant Application Data** *(For Public Bodies like Vejdirektoratet)*
Precise operational figures formatted to match regional and national electrification subsidy application forms.
* **Client Presentation** *(For Management & Municipal Politicians)*
Executive summary deck communicating the core decision, costs, and risks in five clear slides.
