## Company Profile

- **Person(s) quoted:** Rikke Kragh Iversen — områdeleder (area manager) for the municipality's home care
- **Website noted in source:** ishoj.dk

## Electrification Timeline
- Home care's needs and the municipality's geography are described as well-suited to EVs; adoption stems from political will as part of a green agenda.
- **Late 2014:** First EVs acquired.
- Region Hovedstadens elbilsekretariat (Capital Region's EV Secretariat) was involved throughout the decision process. Ishøj Kommune first had the opportunity to trial EVs in daily use via a demonstration package of 4 different EVs and associated charging facilities. This was followed up by the EV secretariat's traveling task force ("rejsehold"), which mapped the existing fleet and analyzed the potential and economics of switching from fossil-based to green transport.
- Given positive experience, more EVs were planned/expected in the following years.

## Operational Notes
- Initially, EVs were unfamiliar and untested territory for staff, requiring some adjustment to silent driving, automatic transmission, and remembering to plug the vehicle in for charging.
- Sentiment shift over time: EVs went from being the vehicles staff avoided to being the most requested vehicles — described as typically the first set of keys taken from the key cabinet at each shift change.
- A promotional film referenced in source: "Ellevilde med elbiler" (roughly "Wild about EVs"), produced by TV Ishøj.

## Quotes
- Rikke Kragh Iversen: "Vi er fuldt ud tilfredse med vores elbiler, medarbejderne er glade for at køre i elbilerne, som foretrækkes af de fleste medarbejdere i forhold til vores konventionelle biler." (We are fully satisfied with our EVs, employees are happy to drive the EVs, which are preferred by most staff over our conventional vehicles.)
- Rikke Kragh Iversen: "Til at starte med var elbilerne de biler der blev gået uden om, men i dag er der en ganske anderledes stemning, nu er elbilerne de fortrukne biler." (At first the EVs were the vehicles people avoided, but today the mood is quite different — now the EVs are the preferred vehicles.)

## Future Plans
- Electric share of the home-care fleet expected to increase over the following years, based on positive initial experience.
