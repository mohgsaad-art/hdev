# 1st Mile / Søren Houmøller — Fleet Electrification Case Study

**Source file:** `Casehistorier_Fagfolk_templafy.pdf` (compendium; story: "Fra sort til grøn transport med en bil der tilhører den elektroniske tidsalder")
**Category:** Case study — individual/private EV adoption (business owner)
**Original language:** Danish (translated to English below; proper nouns kept in Danish)
**One-line summary:** Søren Houmøller, owner-director of energy/environment consultancy 1st Mile, replaced his Audi A5 TDI with a BMW i3 REX (range-extender plug-in hybrid), calling the switch simple common sense, and charges at home via a CLEVER subscription that pays 1 DKK back per kWh consumed.

## Company Profile
- **Individual/Organization:** Søren Houmøller — Virksomhedsejer og direktør (business owner and director) of 1st Mile, an energy- and environment-project consultancy
- **Sector:** Energy/environment consulting
- **Vehicle:** Previously Audi A5 TDI (diesel); switched to BMW i3 REX (Range Extender)
- **Website noted in source:** 1stmile.dk

## Electrification Timeline
- Houmøller previously drove a "prestige" diesel vehicle — an Audi A5 TDI — suited to both family and business needs, but with real running costs.
- As his leasing contract neared expiry, he considered an EV. He had previously thought about EVs but did not want to be a "first mover"; with Volkswagen and BMW entering the market with new models, German-brand EV options became available.
- A test drive of the BMW i3 became the trigger moving him from consideration to action.
- Houmøller's new car became a BMW i3 REX (Range Extender variant) — a plug-in hybrid with a small petrol engine that generates additional electricity to charge the battery while driving, extending range; despite this range-extending capability, Houmøller reports driving most of his kilometers on electric power.

## Operational Notes
- Houmøller describes his first driving impressions of the i3 as fun — the car drives well, silently, with good acceleration, and signals that it belongs to "den elektroniske tidsalder" (the electronic age).
- The Range Extender variant is valued specifically for the psychological reassurance ("ro i maven" — peace of mind) of being able to reach a destination regardless of battery range.
- Charging is described as simple and no different from remembering to charge an iPhone.
- Houmøller values both the driving enjoyment and the greater energy efficiency of an EV vs. an internal-combustion vehicle, and its contribution to reducing CO2 emissions.
