## Appendix 3 — Vehicle height requirements for rigid lorries and tractors (▼M1)

1. Medium rigid lorries, heavy rigid lorries and tractors measured in the constant speed test in accordance with point 3 of this Annex have to meet the vehicle height requirements shown in Table 2.
2. The vehicle height has to be determined as described in 3.5.3.1, item (vii).
3. Any rigid lorries and tractors of vehicle groups not shown in Table 2 are **not subject to constant speed testing**.

## Appendix 4 — Standard body and semi-trailer configurations for rigid lorries and tractors (▼M3 header, ▼B body)

1. **►M3** Medium rigid lorries and heavy rigid lorries subject to air drag determination must fulfil the standard-body requirements of this Appendix. Tractors must fulfil the standard-semitrailer requirements of this Appendix. ◄
2. The applicable standard body or semitrailer shall be determined from **Table 8**.

> **Table-numbering discrepancy in the source text:** point 2 says "determined from Table 8," but the table that immediately follows is explicitly headed "**Table 3** — Allocation of standard bodies and semitrailer for constant speed testing." As with the Table 1/Table 2 discrepancy noted in file `09c`, this is reproduced faithfully from the consolidated text and flagged rather than silently corrected — it appears to be a genuine numbering artefact in the official consolidated document (likely a cross-reference left over from an earlier, differently-numbered version of the Annex).
