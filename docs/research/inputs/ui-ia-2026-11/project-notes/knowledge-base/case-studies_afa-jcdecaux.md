# AFA JCDecaux — Fleet Electrification Case Study

**Source file:** `Casehistorier_Fagfolk_templafy.pdf` (compendium; story: "Stort potentiale i eldrevne servicekøretøjer" — featuring AFA JCDecaux and the TRIPL electric scooter)
**Category:** Case study — fleet electrification (street-furniture/advertising service & maintenance; includes electric scooter and Vehicle-to-Equipment case)
**Original language:** Danish (translated to English below; proper nouns kept in Danish)
**One-line summary:** AFA JCDecaux, which maintains outdoor advertising and street furniture nationwide, achieved 100% CO2-neutral service driving in the Copenhagen metro area by converting to electric vans (with V2E — vehicle-to-equipment — battery packs) and the Danish-made TRIPL electric scooter, cutting operating costs in half via a Vehicle-to-Grid setup with EWII.

## Company Profile
- **Company:** AFA JCDecaux — maintains advertising/street-furniture equipment (bus stops, shelters, train stations) nationwide, including Copenhagen; part of the international JCDecaux group
- **Industry/sector:** Street furniture and outdoor advertising installation & maintenance service
- **Fleet scale:** ca. 60 vehicles nationally (all vehicle types); operates across the country, servicing lufthavne (airports), DSB, Banestyrelsen, and COOP locations
- **Partner/supplier:** EWII — Danish energy company that manufactures and supplies the TRIPL electric scooter and its associated driftspakke (service/maintenance package)
- **Person(s) quoted:** Tonny Pagter — Miljø- og kvalitetschef / Klima- og miljøchef (Environment & Quality Manager / Climate & Environment Manager), AFA JCDecaux; Morten Irming — Salgschef/salgsansvarlig (Sales Manager), EWII

## Electrification Timeline
- Analysis background: a study by Copenhagen Electric found significant socioeconomic potential in converting service vehicles to electric to reduce noise, greenhouse gases, and air pollution in cities.
- AFA JCDecaux began its conversion in partnership with Copenhagen Electric, first acquiring 7 electric Nissan vans, each fitted with an extra battery unit to power the cleaning equipment operators use — a solution described as "(electric) vehicle to equipment (V2E)."
- **Since spring 2016:** AFA JCDecaux has replaced all of its vehicles in the capital region with electric vehicles used daily to service street-furniture advertising equipment.
- The company initially tried replacing some inner-city vehicles with cargo bikes, which provided good exercise for staff but could not carry the required volume of tools and water (each unit needs to transport up to 200 litres of demineralized water), and were not suited to the Tuesday-Friday cleaning rounds.
- **Spring 2017:** first three TRIPL electric scooters (Danish-made, by EWII) delivered as a replacement for the cargo bikes, with further orders following.
- As of the case's writing, AFA JCDecaux is already 100% CO2-neutral for its service driving in the greater Copenhagen area ("AFA JCDecaux er allerede 100% CO2 neutral, når det kommer til servicekørsel i hovedstadsområdet").
- No fixed target year has been set for total fleet conversion nationally, as it depends on market and technology development; the company still operates a few diesel truck/crane vehicles and some vehicles that must cover long distances.

- Operating costs for the TRIPL scooter fleet: reduced to half ("reduceret vores driftsomkostninger til det halve") — no absolute DKK figure given.

## Operational Notes
- V2E (vehicle-to-equipment): extra battery packs in service vans power the cleaning equipment directly, avoiding the need for engine idling.
- TRIPL is chosen specifically for fast inner-city movement with many stops; kørselsradius (driving radius) and proximity to the local depot/lager determine whether a TRIPL or a full electric van is assigned to a given area.
- Weather is a real operational constraint for TRIPL, which is an open (roofless) vehicle; AFA JCDecaux expects only a few days per year will require switching a TRIPL route to an electric van due to poor weather, and had not yet been through a full TRIPL winter at time of writing.
- Safety: TRIPL requires only an ordinary driving license and no special training legally, though EWII runs a "TRIPL Academy" for new users, and AFA JCDecaux runs its own introduction courses and mandates helmet use.
- AFA JCDecaux notes wider European trends favor agile electric scooters like TRIPL for urban service work (e.g. TRIPL may use bus lanes in London and park along building walls in Berlin).

## Quotes
- Tonny Pagter (Miljø- og kvalitetschef): "Vi har ikke fastsat et specifikt årstal for den totale omstilling, fordi det afhænger helt af markedet og hvor hurtigt teknologien udvikler sig... vi vurderer hele tiden, om der er produkter på markedet, som kan erstatte vores dieselkøretøjer, da vi også har nogle få last- og kranbiler og stadig mange biler, der skal kunne køre langt." (We haven't set a specific year for the total conversion, because it depends entirely on the market and how fast the technology develops... we're constantly assessing whether there are products on the market that can replace our diesel vehicles, since we also have a few truck and crane vehicles and still many vehicles that need to be able to drive long distances.)
- Tonny Pagter: "De første syv V2E elbiler er en del af vores strategi for miljøfremmende og energibesparende tiltag, og vores gode erfaringer har allerede ført til beslutning om leasing af flere elbiler til opgaver i byerne. På lidt længere sigt, og når elbilernes rækkevidde tillader det, udskifter vi alle fossilbiler til elbiler. 40% af den kørsel vores chauffører foretager, er ruter under 100 km, som sagtens kunne køre på el." (The first seven V2E EVs are part of our strategy for environmentally promoting and energy-saving measures, and our good experience has already led to a decision to lease more EVs for city tasks. In the somewhat longer term, and once EV range allows it, we will replace all fossil vehicles with EVs. 40% of our drivers' routes are under 100 km, which could easily run on electric.)
- Tonny Pagter: "Vores positive erfaringer fra elbilerne betød, at vi besluttede helt at undgå tomgangskørsel under service, som ellers altid har været nødvendigt. Derfor har vi nu investeret i ekstra 1 kWh batterier i alle servicevogne. Det gælder også de biler der stadig kører på diesel." (Our positive experience with the EVs meant we decided to completely avoid idling during service, which had otherwise always been necessary. So we've now invested in extra 1 kWh batteries in all service vehicles. That also applies to vehicles still running on diesel.)
- Tonny Pagter, on switching from cargo bikes to TRIPL: "Det blev besværligt for vores medarbejdere at gøre pitstops tilbage på centralen for at udskifte vand. Vi så os derfor om efter andre bæredygtige muligheder. Og vores valg faldt på el-scooteren TRIPL, produceret af energiselskabet EWII." (It became cumbersome for our staff to make pit stops back at the depot to refill water. So we looked for other sustainable options, and our choice fell on the TRIPL electric scooter, produced by energy company EWII.)
- Tonny Pagter, on cost: "Når det kommer til TRIPL er det bare bæredygtigt, også økonomisk. Vi har reduceret vores driftsomkostninger til det halve. Vi har købt en driftspakke af EWII, som servicerer og vedligeholder køretøjerne og så er strømmen billig... Hvad angår vores elbiler, kører vi et Vechicle to Grid setup. Det betyder, at vi reelt har gratis strøm og dermed ingen 'brændstofomkostninger'. Jeg kan kun få øje på plusser." (When it comes to TRIPL it's simply sustainable, economically too. We've cut our operating costs in half. We've bought a service package from EWII that services and maintains the vehicles, and the electricity is cheap... As for our EVs, we run a Vehicle-to-Grid setup. That means we effectively have free electricity and thus no "fuel costs." I can only see upsides.)
- Tonny Pagter: "TRIPL har vist sig genial til at komme hurtigt rundt i den indre by, på kortere ruter med mange stop." (TRIPL has proven brilliant for getting around the inner city quickly, on shorter routes with many stops.)
- Tonny Pagter: "Generelt er vores medarbejdere vilde med at køre i eldrevne køretøjer. Det gælder både de større elservicebiler og TRIPL scooteren. Flere har faktisk selv spurgt om mulighed for at køre elektrisk." (Generally our employees are crazy about driving electric vehicles. That applies to both the larger electric service vans and the TRIPL scooter. Several have actually asked themselves about the possibility of driving electric.)
- Morten Irming (EWII): "Det går stærkt hos AFA JCDecaux, hvor der ikke langt fra tanke til handling... der gik kun få uger fra beslutningen om at prøve TRIPL af i København til de første TRIPL blev leveret." (Things move fast at AFA JCDecaux, where there's not far from thought to action... only a few weeks passed from the decision to try TRIPL in Copenhagen to the first TRIPL units being delivered.)
- Morten Irming: "Vi kommer til at se store forandringer i løbet af få år, når elektrificeringen for alvor slår igennem. I London må TRIPL for eksempel køre i busbanen og i Berlin må man parkere TRIPL langs husmure. Hvis København skal indfri sit 2025 klimamål, er det en god ide at lade sig inspirere af, hvad andre europæiske storbyer gør for at få dieselkøretøjerne ud af byen." (We're going to see major changes within a few years as electrification really takes off. In London, for example, TRIPL can use the bus lane, and in Berlin you can park TRIPL along building walls. If Copenhagen is to meet its 2025 climate goal, it's a good idea to take inspiration from what other major European cities are doing to get diesel vehicles out of the city.)

## Future Plans
- Full replacement of remaining fossil vehicles once EV range allows, with 40% of current routes (those under 100 km) identified as easily electrifiable now.
- Continued expansion of the TRIPL electric-scooter fleet, pending further experience through a first full winter of TRIPL operation.
- Reference to Copenhagen's 2025 climate goal as external context/benchmark (Morten Irming's quote), not stated as AFA JCDecaux's own formal target.
