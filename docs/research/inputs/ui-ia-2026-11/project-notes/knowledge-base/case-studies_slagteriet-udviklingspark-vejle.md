# Slagteriet Udviklingspark Vejle — EV Demonstration Case Study

**Source file:** `Casehistorier_Fagfolk_templafy.pdf` (compendium; story: "Iværksættere tager elbilen til sig")
**Category:** Case study — EV demonstration/awareness event (startup/business incubator)
**Original language:** Danish (translated to English below; proper nouns kept in Danish)
**One-line summary:** A Vejle co-working/incubator space for entrepreneurs and startups hosted the "Mød elbilen" demonstration, with tenants using the test EVs for off-site meetings and several testers reporting they now consider switching their own petrol/diesel cars.

## Company Profile
- **Organization:** Slagteriet Udviklingspark Vejle (part of Innovation & Entreprenørskab within Vejle Erhvervsudvikling), together with sister site Roberthus Udviklingspark Egtved
- **Sector:** Business/startup incubator and co-working space, housing entrepreneurs and start-up companies
- **Location:** Vejle — housed in the administration building of the now-demolished Tulip Slagteri (formerly Vejle og Omegns Andels-Svineslagteri), hence the name "Slagteriet" (The Slaughterhouse)
- **Person(s) quoted:** Per Hein — Centerleder (Center Manager) of Slagteriet Udviklingspark Vejle and Roberthus Udviklingspark Egtved, also responsible for a range of entrepreneurship activities within Vejle Kommune Erhverv

## Electrification Timeline
- Slagteriet Udviklingspark Vejle was offered the opportunity to host Region Hovedstaden's "Mød elbilen" demonstration project (4 EVs plus charging stations) and accepted immediately, without hesitation.
- The demonstration was made available to the co-working space's many tenant entrepreneurs and startups, several of whom work in new technology and trends.
- Tenants used the EVs both for genuine off-site meetings/errands and simply to test-drive and familiarize themselves with one of the EV models.

## Operational Notes
- The demonstration generated significant informal discussion ("snak på gangene") throughout the co-working space.
- Several test-drivers reported they are now considering switching their current petrol/diesel cars for an EV.

## Quotes
- Per Hein: "Elbiler er for mange et hot emne og der har været stor nysgerrighed og interesse for at prøve elbilerne og blive klogere på, hvad moderne elbiler kan tilbyde. Alle har været yderst positive." (EVs are a hot topic for many, and there has been great curiosity and interest in trying the EVs and learning more about what modern EVs can offer. Everyone has been extremely positive.)
- Per Hein: "Der er ingen tvivl om, at mødet med elbilerne har gjort indtryk og bidraget til en ændret holdning til hvad en elbil er i dag, jeg selv er blevet overrasket over hvor godt de kører, den lydløse kørsel og hurtige acceleration er meget appellerende." (There's no doubt that meeting the EVs made an impression and contributed to a changed attitude about what an EV is today; I myself was surprised at how well they drive — the silent ride and quick acceleration are very appealing.)
