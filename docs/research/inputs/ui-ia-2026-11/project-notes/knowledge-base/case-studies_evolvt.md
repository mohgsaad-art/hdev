# evolvT — Fleet Electrification Case Study

**Source file:** `CASE_Evolt_FINAL-1.pdf`
**Category:** Case study — fleet electrification (courier/last-mile delivery startup)
**Original language:** Danish (translated to English below; proper nouns kept in Danish)
**One-line summary:** A Copenhagen courier startup, evolvT, built a business model around independent couriers, better working conditions, and an all-electric delivery fleet, with plans to scale internationally.

## Company Profile
- **Company:** evolvT
- **Industry/sector:** Courier / last-mile delivery, connecting small independent couriers directly with customers (bypassing large-platform intermediaries)
- **Fleet size:** 4 electric vans (3 Maxus, 1 Nissan e-NV200)
- **Locations:** Copenhagen (Danish HQ); partners in Paris and Silicon Valley for international expansion
- **Person(s) quoted:** Robert Spaima — CEO; Johan Vang Wildt — Co-founder of customer company Flowering
- **Named customer:** Flowering — a sustainability-focused flower business using only eco-certified, hand-sorted surplus, and locally grown Danish flowers
- **Publication year of case:** 2021

## Electrification Timeline
- No specific founding year or EV-adoption start date given in source.
- Company built around a social/economic mission (fair pay and working conditions for independent couriers) combined with an all-electric vehicle fleet from the outset, described as part of its core value proposition to sustainability-minded customers like Flowering.
- Company states it can scale to 30 vehicles within three months when demand requires it.

## Operational Notes
- Range is described explicitly as "an issue" requiring detailed planning, but professional drivers quickly learn to optimize daily routes around it.
- Charging occurs both overnight and during daytime breaks/pauses.
- Business model: direct contact with customers, bypassing large-platform intermediaries, to give small independent courier businesses better margins and working conditions.
- No V2X or smart-charging/grid details mentioned.
- evolvT is also actively evaluating other green last-mile technologies beyond EVs: e-bikes ("elcykler," described as an obvious next step), delivery drones, and robots.

## Quotes
- Johan Vang Wildt (Flowering): "Vi er en ung start-up virksomhed, og for os er bæredygtighed lige så vigtigt som bundlinjen. Derfor er det afgørende af have en logistikpartner, der vægter både miljø og social bæredygtighed." (We're a young startup, and for us sustainability is as important as the bottom line. That's why it's crucial to have a logistics partner that values both environmental and social sustainability.)
- Robert Spaima: "Rækkevidden er selvfølgelig et issue. En elvarebil kører ca. 250 km på en opladning, så det kræver detaljeret planlægning at få det hele til at gå op. Heldigvis er chaufførerne professionelle og lærer lynhurtigt at optimere kørslen i hverdagen." (Range is of course an issue. An electric van drives about 250 km on one charge, so it requires detailed planning to make it all work out. Fortunately the drivers are professional and learn very quickly to optimize their daily driving.)
- Robert Spaima: "Vi kan skalere op med 30 biler på tre måneder, så vi er klar til at indfri efterspørgslen, når den kommer." (We can scale up by 30 vehicles in three months, so we're ready to meet demand when it comes.)
- Robert Spaima: "Før var det store, potente biler, der kunne få folk til at dreje hovedet i gadebilledet. I dag er det de lydløse elbiler, der fanger interessen." (Before it was big, powerful cars that made people turn their heads in the street. Today it's the silent electric vehicles that catch people's interest.)
- Robert Spaima: "Vi har en vision om at ændre vores industri på globalt plan. Der er millioner af underleverandører, der trænger til bedre vilkår og millioner af tons CO2, som naturen skal skånes for." (We have a vision to change our industry globally. There are millions of subcontractors who need better conditions, and millions of tons of CO2 that nature needs to be spared from.)

## Future Plans
- International expansion, with existing collaboration partners in Paris and Silicon Valley.
- Actively seeking online retailers with matching values to fuel growth in Denmark.
- Exploring e-bikes, delivery drones, and robots as additional green last-mile technologies.
