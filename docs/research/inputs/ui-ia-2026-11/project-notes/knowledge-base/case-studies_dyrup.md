# Dyrup (PPG Industries) — EV Demonstration Case Study

**Source file:** `Casehistorier_Fagfolk_templafy.pdf` (compendium; story: "Grønt besøg hos Dyrup")
**Category:** Case study — EV demonstration/awareness event (paint manufacturing)
**Original language:** Danish (translated to English below; proper nouns kept in Danish)
**One-line summary:** Paint manufacturer Dyrup (part of PPG Industries) hosted the "Mød elbilen" demonstration, with staff praising the acceleration, quiet ride and comfort of test vehicles including a Nissan e-NV200 van and VW e-Golf, though its high-mileage traveling salespeople were judged not yet realistic candidates for full electrification.

## Company Profile
- **Company:** Dyrup (Dyrup A/S), part of PPG Industries since 2012
- **Industry/sector:** Paint and coatings manufacturing
- **Parent company scale:** PPG Industries has 47,000 employees globally, of which 325 are in Scandinavia, with headquarters and production in Søborg
- **Founded:** 1928, by Sigurd Dyrup; original factory a small paint/lacquer plant in Brønshøj; became supplier of rust protection for the first Lillebæltsbro (Little Belt Bridge), then moved to its current Søborg address in 1934
- **Person(s) quoted:** Peter Sørensen — Adm. direktør (Managing Director), Dyrup

## Electrification Timeline
- Dyrup, as part of its consistent brand focus on responsibility, had prior experience with early-generation EV models, which at the time struggled to meet expectations — the "Mød elbilen" demonstration with modern EVs offered a markedly different, more positive experience.
- Opportunity to participate came via Gladsaxe Kommune, with whom Dyrup has an existing collaboration including shared social engagement/CSR activities.
- Three passenger EVs from the demonstration package were offered to administrative staff, many of whom test-drove one or more; the electric Nissan e-NV200 van was used extensively in the Maintenance department (Vedligeholdelsesafdelingen). The Managing Director personally test-drove a Volkswagen e-Golf.
- All participants reported positively, particularly highlighting good handling, fast acceleration, and comfortable, quiet, gearless driving.

## Financial Figures
- No DKK or CO2 figures are stated in this source; the story is qualitative/awareness-focused.

## Operational Notes
- Dyrup considered whether to install a charging station at its site for visiting EV drivers, as a result of the demo's positive reception.
- Dyrup's traveling sales staff drive 40,000–50,000 km/year, including long cross-country trips — deemed not currently realistic for EV replacement at the time of writing ("Elbiler som firmabiler til de kørende sælgere vurderes pt. ikke realistisk").

## Quotes
- Peter Sørensen (Adm. direktør): "Jeg syntes elbilen kørte godt og jeg kan sagtens forestille mig en elbil, men må ærligt sige, at det nok i givet fald vil blive som bil nummer to. Jeg ser frem til, at elbilerne for alvor slår igennem og bliver mere tilgængelige." (I thought the EV drove well and I can easily imagine having an EV, but must honestly say it would probably end up as a second car. I look forward to EVs really breaking through and becoming more accessible.)
- Peter Sørensen: "Mødet med elbilerne har bestemt været positivt og bidraget til et større kendskab og bevidsthed om de grønne alternativer til benzin- og dieselbiler, så det er kun et spørgsmål om tid, før vi vil se elbiler her i virksomheden." (The meeting with the EVs has definitely been positive and contributed to greater knowledge and awareness of the green alternatives to petrol and diesel cars, so it's only a matter of time before we'll see EVs here at the company.)

## Future Plans
- Dyrup anticipates it is "only a matter of time" before EVs are adopted at the company, contingent on further range/market development for its high-mileage sales fleet use case.
- Consideration of installing an on-site charging station for visitors.
