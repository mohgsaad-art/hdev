## Session 2: Noise-Reduced Off-Peak Delivery (Tanja Ballhorn, Københavns Kommune, Teknik- og Miljøforvaltningen, Mobilitet)

### Historical Background
- As part of the **"aftale om grøn transportpolitik"** (Green Transport Policy Agreement) between the Danish government (Venstre (V) and Konservative (K)), Socialdemokraterne, Dansk Folkeparti, Socialistisk Folkeparti, Det Radikale Venstre, and Liberal Alliance, **200 million DKK** was allocated for trials in **2010–2013**.
- **Teknologisk Institut** (Danish Technological Institute) applied to **Trafikstyrelsens Grønne Pulje** (Danish Transport Authority's Green Pool/fund) on behalf of a consortium for 4 trials:
  - **Lantmännen Schulstad**
  - **Carlsberg**
  - **Dansk Retursystem**
  - Waste handling, **M. Larsen**

### Københavns Kommune's Position and Rationale
Københavns Kommune states it is in the process of translating the national guidance into a municipal guidance document ("kommunal vejledning"). Five stated reasons ("Der er mange gode grunde til at flytte en del af vareleveringen i ydertimerne") to shift part of goods delivery to off-peak hours:
1. Many trucks and vans moved away from rush hour, reducing rush-hour congestion.
2. Faster deliveries by utilizing spare capacity on the road network.
3. Better traffic safety and sense of security for cyclists and pedestrians.
4. Reduction in CO2 and particulate emissions through increased efficiency.
5. Economic savings for transport companies and, through them, their transport customers.

### Definition of "Støjsvag Varelevering" (Noise-Reduced Goods Delivery)
Defined as goods delivery meeting all of the following:
- All equipment and vehicles that could cause noise are certified by **Piek-Keur** (www.piek-international.com).
- Drivers are trained and informed about noise-reduced goods delivery.
- Store employees participating in goods receipt are trained and informed about noise-reduced goods delivery.
Stated explicitly: currently only **one internationally recognized certification scheme exists** — the Dutch **Piek-Keur**.
