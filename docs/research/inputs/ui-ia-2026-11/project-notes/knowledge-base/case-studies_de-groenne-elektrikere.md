# De Grønne Elektrikere — Fleet Electrification Case Study

**Source file:** `Casehistorier_Fagfolk_templafy.pdf` (compendium; story: "Det cykler derud af med elbiler hos de grønne elektrikere")
**Category:** Case study — fleet electrification (electrical installation trade)
**Original language:** Danish (translated to English below; proper nouns kept in Danish)
**One-line summary:** A Copenhagen green electrical-installation business that started in 2009 by having electricians travel by bicycle instead of diesel vans later supplemented its bicycles with two electric Nissan e-NV200 vans to cover a larger service area.

## Company Profile
- **Company:** De Grønne Elektrikere (The Green Electricians), an independent unit under LTECH A/S
- **Industry/sector:** Electrical installation services
- **Fleet:** 1 first electric van, later supplemented with 2 Nissan e-NV200 vans; plus bicycles used by electricians for city-center jobs
- **Location:** Copenhagen (city centre operations)
- **Person(s) quoted:** Jesper Dannisøe — installer and daily manager (daglig leder) of De Grønne Elektrikere
- **Website noted in source:** degroenneelektrikere.dk

## Electrification Timeline
- **2009:** De Grønne Elektrikere established as a standalone unit under LTECH A/S, motivated by a wish to reduce the company's CO2 footprint and meet growing customer demand for energy-conscious solutions.
- **From Whitsun (Pinsen) 2009:** Two electricians began servicing customers in Copenhagen's city centre by bicycle instead of diesel vans ("Dieselbilerne var kørt i garagen").
- Bicycles were later supplemented with electric vans (year not explicitly stated) once the company needed to service a larger geographic area beyond what cycling could cover.
- The first electric van was supplemented with two new Nissan e-NV200 vans.

## Fleet & Charging Technical Details
| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| EV model | 2 Nissan e-NV200 (supplementing 1 earlier electric van) |
| Vehicle fit-out | Shelving/racking installed like any other work van; exterior decorated in company's green branding |
| Depot charging | Charging box from CleanCharge at the company address |
| On-the-road charging | E.ON public charging stations used when needed away from base |
| Financing | Vehicles are leased, so all costs are known in advance |
| Subsidy | Financial support obtained via Region Hovedstadens elbilpartnerskab (Capital Region's EV Partnership) |

## Financial Figures
- No specific DKK savings or investment total is stated in this source; only that leasing gives known/fixed costs and that the partnership's financial subsidy was an incentive to acquire the electric vans ("Det økonomiske tilskud gav tilskyndelse til at anskaffe de elektriske varevogne").
- No CO2 tonnage figure is stated in this source.

## Operational Notes
- The company operates a hybrid model of bicycles (for tight city-centre jobs) and electric vans (for a larger geographic radius).
- Charging: home-base charging via a CleanCharge box; public E.ON chargers used on the road as needed.
- The vans fully meet operational needs and range requirements for the company's customer base, according to the daily manager.
- There are ongoing considerations ("overvejelser") about adding another electric van to the fleet.

## Quotes
- Jesper Dannisøe: "De elektriske varevogne opfylder alle vores behov og vi kan fint komme omkring til vores kunder med elbilernes rækkevidde." (The electric vans meet all our needs and we can easily get around to our customers within the EVs' range.)
- Jesper Dannisøe: "Vores elvarevogne kører fantastisk godt og giver en afstressende kørsel med dejlig ro i kabinen, som man ikke finder i de traditionelle diesel varevogne." (Our electric vans drive fantastically well and give a de-stressing drive with a lovely calm in the cabin, which you don't find in traditional diesel vans.)

## Future Plans
- Consideration of adding a further electric van to the fleet.
- Source notes other trades businesses can quickly get started with electric vans via the "Håndværkerbil på el" (Tradesman's vehicle on electric) project.
