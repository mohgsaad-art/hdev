# Danmarks Tekniske Universitet (DTU) — Fleet Electrification Case Study

**Source file:** `Casehistorier_Fagfolk_templafy.pdf` (compendium; story: "Elbiler og elværktøj i høj kurs på Danmarks Tekniske Universitet")
**Category:** Case study — fleet & equipment electrification (university campus operations)
**Original language:** Danish (translated to English below; proper nouns kept in Danish)
**One-line summary:** DTU (Technical University of Denmark) uses electric pool cars for off-campus meetings and electric mini-loaders plus electric grounds tools across its 700,000+ m² campus, converting Park & Vej staff from skepticism to enthusiasm.

## Company Profile
- **Organization:** Danmarks Tekniske Universitet (DTU) — Park & Vej (Parks & Roads) department specifically featured
- **Sector:** Higher education / campus operations
- **Fleet:** 3 Nissan Leaf (pool cars, bookable by rector and administration for off-campus meetings), 3 electric mini-loaders ("el. mini lastere")
- **Campus size:** More than 700,000 m²
- **Person(s) quoted:** Michel Kruse Jepsen (also referred to once as "Michael Kruse Jepsen" in source) — Sektionsleder (Section Leader), Park & Vej department

## Electrification Timeline
- No specific start date given.
- Rector and administration can book an electric pool car (Nissan Leaf) for off-campus meetings, avoiding fossil fuel combustion and signaling that "electric vehicles are here now and the future of transport."
- Charging stations are installed around campus, available to both DTU's own vehicles and visitors' EVs.
- Park & Vej department also uses 3 electric mini-loaders for maintaining roads and green areas across campus, plus electric hedge trimmers and lawnmowers ("elektriske hække- og plæneklippere").
- Some Park & Vej staff were initially skeptical of the electric mini-loaders and electric grounds tools, but skepticism turned to enthusiasm after trying the equipment.

## Operational Notes
- Electric grounds tools are lighter than petrol equivalents, reducing physical strain on operators, and produce no noise or exhaust fumes — specifically valued because Park & Vej works close to buildings with active teaching/lecture halls where petrol-tool noise was previously disruptive.
- Snow removal is noted as a current challenge for electric vehicles, given large snow volumes and the fact that clearing must happen around the clock — but described as "just a matter of time" before this becomes feasible too.
- Stated operating principle: "Vi overgår til el, der hvor det er muligt og anvendeligt" (We convert to electric wherever it's possible and applicable).
