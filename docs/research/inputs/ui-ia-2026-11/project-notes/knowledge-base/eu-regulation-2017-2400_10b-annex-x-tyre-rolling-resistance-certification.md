## 1. Introduction

Annex X describes the **certification provisions for tyres with regard to their rolling resistance coefficient**. For calculation of the vehicle rolling resistance to be used as a simulation-tool (VECTO) input, the applicable **tyre rolling resistance coefficient Cr** for each tyre type supplied to the original equipment manufacturers (OEMs), and the related **tyre test load F_ZTYRE**, shall be declared by the applicant for pneumatic-tyre approval.

## 3. General requirements

### 3.1. Plant certification

The tyre manufacturer plant shall be certified to **►M3 IATF ◄ 16949** (IATF 16949 is the automotive-sector quality-management-system standard; prior to the ▼M3 amendment this likely referenced a different/older designation — the amendment marker shows "IATF" itself was inserted/corrected by M3).

*[amendment boundary: ▼M3]*

### 3.2. Tyre rolling resistance coefficient measurement

The tyre rolling resistance coefficient shall be:
- **measured and aligned in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2020/740, Annex I, part A**,
- **expressed in N/kN**,
- **rounded to the first decimal place**, in accordance with **ISO 80000-1 Appendix B, section B.3, rule B (example 1)**.

**Standard (default) rolling resistance coefficient values:**
- For **C2 and C3 tyres**: the standard value shall be the one corresponding to **snow tyres for use in severe snow conditions** as set out in **UN Regulation No. 117, paragraph 6.3.2**.
- For tyres **not in the scope of Regulation (EC) No 661/2009 or Regulation (EU) 2019/2144**: the standard value shall be **13.0 N/kN** and the **FuelEfficiencyClass** shall be stated as **"N/A"**.

**Standard F_zISO value:**
- The standard F_zISO value shall be the one obtained as a **percentage of the vertical force related to tyre load index** at nominal tyre pressure (and single tyre application).
  - For **C2 and C3 tyres**: this percentage shall be **85%**.
  - For **other tyres**: the percentage shall be **80%**.

Footnote references:
- Regulation (EC) No 661/2009 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 July 2009 concerning type-approval requirements for the general safety of motor vehicles, their trailers and systems, components and separate technical units intended therefor (OJ L 200, 31.7.2009, p. 1).
- Regulation (EU) 2019/2144 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 November 2019 on type-approval requirements for motor vehicles and their trailers, and systems, components and separate technical units intended for such vehicles, as regards their general safety and the protection of vehicle occupants and vulnerable road users, amending Regulation (EU) 2018/858 (OJ L 325, 16.12.2019, p. 1).

### 3.3. Measurement provisions

The tyre manufacturer shall test **either**:
- in a laboratory of **technical services** as defined in **Article 68 of Regulation (EU) 2018/858**, the test referred to in point 3.2; **or**

  - (i) a representative of a technical service designated by the responsible approval authority **supervises** the test; or
  - (ii) the tyre manufacturer is designated as a **technical service of Category A** in accordance with Article 68 of Regulation (EU) 2018/858.

## 4. Conformity of the certified CO2 emissions and fuel consumption related properties

**4.1.** Any tyre certified under this Regulation shall be **in conformity** with the declared rolling resistance value as per paragraph 3.2 of this Annex.

**4.2.** In order to verify conformity of the certified CO2 emissions and fuel consumption related properties, **production samples shall be taken randomly from series production** and tested in accordance with the provisions set out in paragraph 3.2. ►M3 The tests have to be performed on **new test tyres** in the sense of the definition set out in paragraph 2 of UN Regulation No. 117. ◄

### 4.3. Frequency of the tests

**4.3.1.** The tyre rolling resistance of **at least one tyre** of a specific type intended for sales to the original equipment manufacturers shall be tested **every 20,000 units of this type per year** (e.g. **2 conformity verifications per year** for a type whose annual sales volume to OEMs is between 20,001 and 40,000 units).

**4.3.2.** Where deliveries of a specific tyre type intended for sales to OEMs are **between 500 and 20,000 units per year**, at least **one conformity verification per year** shall be carried out.

**4.3.3.** Where deliveries of a specific tyre type intended for sales to OEMs are **below 500 units**, at least one conformity verification as described in paragraph 4.4 shall be applied **every second year**.

**4.3.4.** If the volume threshold indicated in 4.3.1 is met **within 31 calendar days**, the maximum number of conformity verifications described in paragraph 4.3 is limited to **one per 31 calendar days**.

**4.3.5.** The manufacturer shall **justify** (e.g. by showing sales numbers) to the approval authority the number of tests performed.

### 4.4. Verification procedure

**4.4.1.** A single tyre shall be tested in accordance with paragraph 3.2. **By default, the machine alignment equation shall be the one valid at the date of verification testing.** ►M3 __________ ◄ *(the ►M3 marker here indicates text was deleted at this point by Regulation (EU) 2022/1379, with no replacement text shown in the consolidated version — rendered in the source as a blank underscore placeholder)*

*[amendment boundary: ▼M3]*

**4.4.2.** In the case that the **measured and aligned value is lower or equal to the declared value plus 0.3 N/kN**, the tyre rolling resistance value is considered **compliant**.

**4.4.3.** Where the measured and aligned value **exceeds the declared value by more than 0.3 N/kN**, the **alignment equation that was valid at the time of the certification testing** may be applied, upon request of the tyre manufacturer and in agreement with the authority supervising the verification.

**4.4.3.1.** If the measured and **re-aligned** value is lower or equal to the declared value plus **0.3 N/kN**, the tyre rolling resistance value is considered **compliant**.

**4.4.3.2.** If the measured value, aligned as per points 4.4.3 and 4.4.3.1, **exceeds the declared value by more than 0.3 N/kN**, **three additional tyres shall be tested**. If the measured value (aligned as per 4.4.3/4.4.3.1) of **at least one of the three tyres exceeds the declared value by more than 0.4 N/kN**, **Article 23 shall apply** *(Article 23 of the main Regulation body — non-conformity/penalty provisions; not part of this Annex and not reproduced here)*.

> **Verification tolerance summary table (derived from 4.4.2/4.4.3/4.4.3.2 — not a table in the source, constructed here for clarity; underlying numeric thresholds are verbatim from the source text):**
>
> | Deviation of measured (aligned) Cr from declared Cr | Outcome |
> |---|---|
> | ≤ +0.3 N/kN | Compliant |
> | > +0.3 N/kN | May re-align using the certification-date alignment equation (by manufacturer request + authority agreement); if re-aligned value ≤ +0.3 N/kN → compliant |
> | > +0.3 N/kN even after re-alignment | Test 3 additional tyres |
> | Any of the 3 additional tyres > +0.4 N/kN (aligned) | Article 23 (non-conformity) applies |

## Key numeric thresholds and formulas (verbatim, for quick reference)

1. **Cr rounding/expression:** N/kN, rounded to first decimal place, per ISO 80000-1 Appendix B §B.3 rule B (example 1).
2. **Standard/default Cr for C2 & C3 tyres:** the value corresponding to severe-snow-condition snow tyres per UN Reg. 117 §6.3.2 (no single numeric value given in this Annex — it references the UN Reg. 117 snow-tyre value).
3. **Standard/default Cr for tyres outside scope of Reg. (EC) 661/2009 / Reg. (EU) 2019/2144:** **13.0 N/kN** (explicit numeric value), with FuelEfficiencyClass = "N/A".
4. **Standard F_zISO percentage:** 85% (C2/C3 tyres) or 80% (other tyres) of the vertical force related to tyre load index at nominal pressure, single-tyre application.
5. **Conformity tolerance (initial check):** measured & aligned Cr ≤ declared Cr + **0.3 N/kN** → compliant.
6. **Conformity tolerance (after re-alignment):** re-aligned Cr ≤ declared Cr + **0.3 N/kN** → compliant.
7. **Escalation to 3-tyre retest:** triggered when re-aligned Cr > declared Cr + **0.3 N/kN**.
8. **Failure threshold on retest:** any of the 3 additional tyres with aligned Cr > declared Cr + **0.4 N/kN** → Article 23 (non-conformity consequences) applies.
9. **Test frequency:** 1 test per 20,000 units/year (prorated, e.g. 2/year for 20,001–40,000 units/year); 1 test/year minimum for 500–20,000 units/year; 1 test every 2 years for <500 units/year; capped at 1 test per 31 calendar days regardless.
