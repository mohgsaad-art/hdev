## Appendix 5 — Air drag family (▼M3 title)

### 1. General (▼B, with ►M4 amendment)

An air drag family is characterised by design and performance parameters, common to all vehicles within the family. **►M4** The manufacturer may decide which vehicles belong to an air drag family, as long as the membership criteria listed in point 4 (medium/heavy lorries) and point 6 (heavy buses) are respected. The air drag family shall be approved by the approval authority. ◄ *(Note: the source text repeats "The air drag family shall be approved by the approval authority" immediately after the M4-amended sentence — this duplication is reproduced exactly as it appears in the consolidated text, not an extraction artefact.)* The manufacturer shall provide to the approval authority the appropriate information relating to the air drag of the members of the air drag family.

### 2. Special cases (▼B, with ▼M3 addition)

In some cases there may be interaction between parameters. This shall be taken into consideration to ensure that only vehicles with similar characteristics are included within the same air drag family. These cases shall be identified by the manufacturer and notified to the approval authority, and then taken into account as a criterion for creating a new air drag family.

**▼M3**
In addition to the parameters listed in point 4 of this Appendix (medium and heavy lorries) and point 6.1 of this Appendix (heavy buses), the manufacturer may introduce additional criteria allowing the definition of families of more restricted size.

**▼M1** Point 3 of this Appendix was deleted (shown as `__________` in the consolidated text, i.e. no replacement text).

### 4. Parameter defining the air drag family for medium and heavy lorries (▼M3 heading)

**4.1.** **►M3** Medium and heavy lorries are allowed to be grouped within a family if they belong to the same vehicle group according to **Table 1 or Table 2 of Annex I** and the following criteria are fulfilled: ◄

(a) **Same cabin width and body-in-white geometry** up to B-pillar and above the heel point, excluding the cab bottom (e.g. engine tunnel). All family members stay within **± 10 mm** of the parent vehicle.

(b) **Same roof height in vertical Z.** All family members stay within **± 10 mm** of the parent vehicle.

(c) **►M3** For vehicles with a frame: same height of cabin over frame. ◄ This criterion is fulfilled if the height difference of the cabins over frame stays within **Z < 175 mm**.

The fulfilment of the family concept requirements shall be demonstrated by **CAD (computer-aided design) data**.

**4.2.** An air drag family consists of **testable members** and **vehicle configurations which cannot be tested** in accordance with this Regulation.

**4.3.** Testable members of a family are vehicle configurations which fulfil the installation requirements defined in point 3.3 of the main part of this Annex.

**▼M4**

**4.4.** For vehicles equipped with dynamic charging technologies referred to in Annex III, the following applies:

(a) Vehicles equipped with **overhead pantographs** shall be represented in the aerodynamic configuration with the overhead pantograph in the **retracted** position.

(b) Vehicles equipped with **trolley poles** or devices related to **ground-rail and wireless dynamic charging** may be represented **without** the related devices that allow dynamic recharging.

### 6. Parameter defining the air drag family for heavy buses

**6.1.** Heavy buses are allowed to be grouped within a family if they belong to the same vehicle group according to **Tables 4, 5 and 6 of Annex I**, and the following criteria are fulfilled:

(a) **Vehicle width:** all family members stay within **± 50 mm** of the parent vehicle. Body width determined per the definitions in Annex III.

(b) **Height of the integrated body:** all family members stay within a **total range of 250 mm**. Determined per the definitions in Annex III.

(c) **Vehicle length:** all family members stay within a **total range of 5 m**. Determined per the definitions in Annex III.

Fulfilment of the family concept requirements shall be demonstrated by **computer-aided design data or drawings**; the demonstration method is chosen by the manufacturer.

### 7. Choice of the air drag parent vehicle for heavy buses

The parent vehicle of each family shall be selected per the following criteria:

**▼M3**

**7.1.** All family members shall have an **equal or lower** air drag value than the value Cd·Adeclared for the parent vehicle.

**7.2.** The applicant shall be able to demonstrate that the parent vehicle selection meets the provisions of 7.1, based on scientific methods, e.g. **computational fluid dynamics, wind tunnel results, or good engineering practice**. This demonstration shall cover the influence of **roof-mounted systems**. As tyres are part of the measurement equipment, their influence shall be **excluded** in proving the worst-case scenario.

**7.3.** The declared value Cd·Adeclared can be used for creating families in other sub-groups if the family criteria of point 1 of this Appendix are met, based on **transfer functions or provisions in Table 16b**. Multiple combinations of copy and transfer functions are allowed.

For vehicles of sub-groups labelled **'no'** in the second column of Table 16b, generic (standard) air drag values are allocated automatically by the simulation tool.
