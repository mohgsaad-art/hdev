# Unicon — Fleet Electrification Case Study

**Source file:** `CASE_2023_Unicon.pdf`
**Category:** Case study — fleet electrification (heavy-duty electric concrete mixer trucks)
**One-line summary:** Denmark's largest concrete producer, Unicon, built the world's first series-produced electric concrete mixer truck with Volvo Trucks and Liebherr-Mischtechnik, and is gradually electrifying its 170-truck fleet toward zero-emission construction-site delivery by 2035.
**Original language:** Danish (translated to English below; proper nouns kept in Danish)

## Company Profile
- **Company:** Unicon
- **Industry/sector:** Concrete production and distribution ("Danmarks største producent af beton" — Denmark's largest concrete producer)
- **Fleet size:** 170 concrete mixer trucks total (betonbiler), being gradually electrified; 10 electric units in operation with more arriving continuously (as of 2023)
- **Location:** Fabrikken (factory/plant) at Avedøre (where the featured truck is charged)
- **Partners:** Volvo Trucks, Liebherr-Mischtechnik
- **Person(s) quoted:** Christian Elleby — Supply Chain & Procurement Director; Kurt Pedersen, age 73 — driver/test driver of the electric concrete mixer truck
- **Publication year of case:** 2023

## Electrification Timeline
- **2018:** Unicon set an ambitious strategy to reduce CO2 emissions in distribution, including gradual electrification of its 170-truck concrete-mixer fleet.
- Unicon partnered closely with Volvo Trucks and Liebherr-Mischtechnik to build the world's first series-produced electric concrete mixer truck.
- **2021:** Unicon put the world's first series-produced electric concrete mixer truck on the road.
- **As of 2023 (case publication):** 10 more electric units are in operation, with more arriving continuously ("flere kommer løbende til").
- **Target: 2035** — all concrete mixer trucks to be emission-free ("emissionsfri").

## Fleet & Charging Technical Details
| Spec | Value |
|---|---|
| Model | Volvo FM Electric with concrete mixer superstructure (med opbygning) |
| Curb weight (egenvægt) | 21.6 tonnes |
| Gross vehicle weight (totalvægt) | 44 tonnes |
| Battery capacity | 540 kWh |
| Range | Up to 300 km |
| Consumption per 100 km (incl. power to concrete drum/mixer) | 200 kWh |
| Depot charger power | 43 kW AC (three-phase) |
| Maximum charging power | 250 kW DC |
| Charging time at 43 kW AC (three-phase) | 9.5 hours |
| Charging time at 250 kW DC | 2.5 hours |

- Charging pattern: trucks drive during the day and charge overnight at the Avedøre factory, when electricity is typically cheapest and greenest, and the factory's own production is also idle — meaning no need to increase the site's electrical supply capacity, avoiding additional grid-connection/ampere-upgrade costs.
- The 300 km range is stated to be sufficient to cover a full day's driving for Unicon's use case.

## Financial Figures
- No specific DKK investment, cost, or savings figures are stated in this source.
- No explicit CO2 tonnage-saved figure is given for the fleet in this source (unlike other cases in this knowledge base). The environmental benefit is described qualitatively (better energy utilization by electric motors, reduced local air/noise pollution) rather than quantified in tons/year.
- Avoided cost: Unicon was able to avoid the cost of installing additional electrical supply capacity (ampere) for its charger, since night-time charging coincides with idle factory production.

## Operational Notes
- Driving pattern: "Kører om dagen. Lader om natten" (Drives during the day, charges at night).
- No additional electrical infrastructure upgrade was needed because charging occurs during the factory's own production downtime at night.
- Business driver: Unicon frames this as a responsibility to take an active leadership role in decarbonizing heavy transport, given its market position as Denmark's largest concrete producer, rather than waiting for ready-made solutions to emerge ("Grøn omstilling inden for tung transport er en vanskelig proces, fordi løsningerne ikke ligger klare endnu... Det får dog ikke os til at sætte os på sidelinjen og vente på løsningerne").
- Quality benefit noted: electric motors are said to use energy more efficiently and deliver more power/torque to the mixing drum, resulting in better concrete quality.
- Reliability: according to Christian Elleby, there has not been a single day the electric truck has been out of service since launch ("der har ikke været én eneste dag, hvor bilen har stået stille").
- No V2X or smart-charging details mentioned beyond the note that night charging uses cheaper/greener grid electricity.

## Future Plans
- Target: all Unicon concrete mixer trucks emission-free by 2035.
- Continued rollout of electric units beyond the 10 already in service (more "coming continuously").
- Continued strategic collaboration with Volvo Trucks and Liebherr-Mischtechnik to further develop and implement electric solutions for the concrete industry.
