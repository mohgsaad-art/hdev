# Nordisk Dialogkonference 2023 — New Business Models for Electrifying Commercial Transport (Copenhagen Electric / Region Hovedstaden)

**Source file:** `Praesentationerne-fra-Nordisk-Dialogkonference-29.3.2023_Cph-Electric.pdf`
**Category:** Conference presentation compilation — commercial/HD vehicle electrification business models, charging technology, fleet-management SaaS
**Original language:** Mixed — Danish (conference framing, several Danish/Nordic vendor talks) and English (several vendor talks, esp. German/Swedish presenters); reproduced/translated to English below, proper nouns and Danish quotes kept as in source
**One-line summary:** Slide deck from a one-day "Nordic Dialogue Conference" held in Copenhagen on **29 March 2023**, organized by **Copenhagen Electric** (Region Hovedstaden's regional e-mobility knowledge center), in which nine external speakers/companies pitched business models, charging hardware, battery/PTO systems, and fleet-management software aimed at electrifying road-freight and commercial-vehicle transport — directly relevant to HDEV depot/charging business-model design.

## Document Nature and Extraction Notes
This is a **conference slide-deck compilation**, not a single authored report — it is a straight export of presentation slides from multiple independent companies plus the host organization, stitched together in running order. Content is consequently uneven in depth and register: some slides are dense bullet lists, some are single marketing taglines, some are bare section-divider slides (speaker name + time slot only), and one slide (Idé-redskab #1) appears to be an image-only slide whose text did not extract. No charts/graphs/infographics embedded as images could be extracted as data (e.g., CargoTruckExchange's "PROBLEM & MULIGHED" quadrant graphic, Vialumina's customer-journey graphic, and Uptimus's utilization-by-weekday graphic are described by their visible on-slide labels only, not by underlying chart values). Where a figure or claim is a single vendor's own marketing claim (not an independently verified fact), this is noted explicitly.

## Opening Framing (Copenhagen Electric / Christina Bech Godskesen Andersen)
- **National climate target cited:** 70% CO2 reduction in Denmark by 2030 (consistent with the same national target cited in the Horsens, Esbjerg, and Køge municipal charging-strategy documents already in this knowledge base).
- **Freight-transport emissions share cited:** "Godstransporten står for ca. 10% af den samlede udledning (i Danmark) — stigende mod 20% i 2030" (Freight transport accounts for approx. 10% of Denmark's total emissions, rising toward 20% by 2030). **Note:** this figure is given only as a bare slide bullet with no cited source/methodology in the deck itself, and should be treated as the event organizer's own framing statement rather than an independently sourced statistic.
- **Technology framing (verbatim, translated):** "Necessary — but (still a little) expensive"; "changes the whole ecosystem around transport"; "demands rethinking."
- **Thematic structure of the day:** organized around three overlapping pillars shown in a Venn-style slide: **Målsætning** (Target/ambition), **Teknologi** (Technology), and **Nye forretningsmodeller og løsninger** (New business models and solutions) — with a sub-split of the latter into **Elektrificering** (Electrification) and **Smart (optimering og besparelse)** (Smart — optimization and savings).

## Talk 1 — "Future Transport" (Pernille Lytzen, tGrace)
- **Speaker background:** Founder/Owner, tGrace. Education: B.Sc. & M.Sc. in Business Economics & Psychology, Copenhagen Business School. Prior roles (per slide, translated): Business Development Director (part of management team), Innovation & Business Design Manager, Project Manager, Business Development Consultant.
- **Core message:** Frames near-future mobility technology trends (autonomous driving, drones, cross-domain mobility) as a lens for discovering new business-model thinking today, rather than as a literal roadmap.
- **5-year outlook (per slide, verbatim themes):** advanced driver-assist systems paving the way to full self-driving; growing real autonomous-driving kilometers; improved LIDAR technology; autonomous freight gaining ground quickly; drones achieving greater adoption; cross-domain mobility; type-approval & EU legislation catching up; OEMs entering the space; cross-industry partnerships.
- **10-year outlook (per slide, verbatim):** "Electric, autonomous, door-to-door, on-demand. Smaller, mixed fleet solving tasks across domains. New ecosystem."
- **Cross-cutting takeaways offered to the audience (verbatim themes):** multimodality; much smaller fleets; a new ecosystem around transport; markets merging and influencing each other (and hence new partnership constellations); entirely new markets and services; bundling of services. Common thread across new technologies: "there are more obstacles than open roads"; be deliberate about which problem you are actually trying to solve; use an MVP approach with fast prototyping, fast testing, fast iteration; look outside your own industry and its usual logic.

## Talk 5 — CargoTruckExchange (Henrik Larsen), 11.45–12.00
- **Product:** A SaaS/digital marketplace platform for **freight forwarders (speditører)** to match spare cargo capacity and empty trucks/trailers across internal, private (trusted-partner), and public networks.
- **Value proposition (verbatim, translated):** greater transparency in trading vehicle and cargo capacity (better decision basis, less irrelevant information); lower transport costs (fewer empty kilometres, higher capacity utilization); lower administrative costs (less manual work, fewer human errors).
- **Climate framing, cited from the company's own whitepaper** ("Et styrket transportsystem kan reducere tab af kapacitet og føre til billigere og renere transport, klima og miljø" — "A strengthened transport system can reduce capacity loss and lead to cheaper and cleaner transport, climate and environment"):
  - "Road freight transport emits three million tonnes of CO2 — or 5.9% of Denmark's total emissions."
  - "…the 5.9% can be brought down to 4.9% by reducing empty running from the stated 10% to 5%, while simultaneously increasing the load factor from 12 to 14 tonnes."
  - **Note:** these figures are attributed to CargoTruckExchange's own whitepaper, not to a government or independent statistical source, and are reproduced here as the company's cited claim.
- **Market inefficiency data cited (source not specified in the slide beyond "average" — appears to be European road-freight benchmark data, not Danish-specific):**
  - **1 in 4 kilometres driven empty** ("Europa" — Europe), average
  - **43% average idle/unused capacity** on loaded vehicles in Europe
  - Sectors flagged with high idle capacity/low digitalization on the accompanying (non-extractable) quadrant graphic: Hotel, Knowledge services ("Videnservice"), Construction ("Bygge & Anlæg"), Information & Communication, Travel & Cleaning ("Rejse & Rengøring")
- **Platform mechanics (7 stages, verbatim, translated):**
  1. **Request (Forespørgsel):** dispatcher requests goods/spare capacity via the platform, automatically (via integration) or manually; all requests are anonymous so the dispatcher does not expose their customer as a sales lead.
  2. **Matching:** algorithm matches supply and demand; a match triggers a notification to both responsible dispatchers, prioritized across internal/private/public network.
  3. **Negotiation:** price can be negotiated directly on the platform; all open negotiations are shown together for better overview.
  4. **CMR Fragtbrev (CMR consignment note):** can be exchanged automatically upon a match via the platform.
  5. **Route flexibility:** platform also surfaces off-route goods, widening the effective market.
  6. **Payment:** guaranteed automatic payment — funds transferred to the vehicle owner after 3 days, and collected from the goods owner on a running-month-plus-30-days basis; includes a standardized legal error-handling process (no separate debt-collection/"inkasso" process needed), improving liquidity.
  7. **CMR insurance:** all platform participants must upload their CMR insurance policy, visible to counterparties they wish to exchange goods/vehicles with.
  8. **CO2 calculator:** automatic daily/weekly/monthly CO2-emission reporting, letting the forwarder document caused CO2 emissions to individual customers and to the public.
- **Business Case 1 — fewer kilometres driven:** studies by CargoTruckExchange among "Top 5" European freight-forwarding companies show an **average 19% reduction in kilometres driven** (attributed to fewer empty km and better dispatcher overview). **Note: vendor-conducted study; methodology/sample not detailed in the slide.**
- **Business Case 2 — administrative savings:** measured **12.5% reduction in dispatcher time cost**, based on time studies at two Top-5 forwarders using TIMOCOM and private (email) networks; covered tasks: incoming offers, outgoing offers, price negotiation, invoicing; costed at a **DKK 520/hour** labor rate. Quoted figures: current time-cost index 1,000 → future (with platform) 804 → saving of 196 (i.e., ~19.6% reduction in this specific cost index, though the company separately states "12.5%" as the headline reduction figure — both figures reproduced as given, not reconciled by Claude).
- **Network model:** matches internal network (own dispatching capacity) → private network (trusted partners) → public network ("the rest of the world"). Example slide references "your DFDS colleagues" — suggesting DFDS is a customer or partner used as the illustrative example.
- No named individual contact details given beyond presenter "Henrik Larsen" and the closing line "På vegne af CargoTruckExchange teamet" (On behalf of the CargoTruckExchange team).

## Relevance Notes for HDEV/Depot Tool (Claude's own framing, not from source)
- This deck is the **most vendor/technology-dense single document** in the knowledge base to date for HD-charging-hardware benchmarking: Designwerk's spec sheet (500 kW drive power, 170–900 kWh battery range, 44 kW AC/350 kW DC charging, CCS Type 2 @ 800V) and Nerve Smart Systems' battery-buffered 350 kW charging station (200 kWh BESS, stated 12–18 month ROI) are both concrete, named-vendor data points — useful as one input among several for cross-checking assumptions in vehicle/charger sizing, though every figure here is a **vendor's own marketing/spec claim**, not an independently audited or regulator-published figure, and should be corroborated against a datasheet or type-approval document before being used as a hard input.
- Banke ApS's electric-PTO product line is a reminder that "HDEV depot electrification" is not only about the traction battery/charger — refuse trucks, cranes, and hook-lift bodies commonly carry their own separate PTO power/battery system (17–86 kWh range across their product line), which is a **separate load and separate charging/duty-cycle consideration** from the main traction battery when sizing a depot's total charging demand for a mixed HD fleet.
- CargoTruckExchange's and Dora Nordic's figures (43% average idle capacity, 1-in-4 empty km in Europe; DORA's CO2/km-l/CAN-bus data model) are useful **utilization and telematics-data-schema references** for any module of the tool that models real-world duty cycles or CO2 accounting for depot fleets, but both are vendor-stated, Europe-wide averages with no cited primary statistical source in this deck — they should not be treated as Denmark-specific or as independently verified.
- Given the deck's format (conference slides, several sections image-only or spoken-only), this document is best treated as a **directory of potentially relevant vendors and a rough technology/business-model landscape snapshot as of March 2023**, rather than as a primary technical or regulatory source — follow-up should go to each vendor's own datasheets, or to Vejdirektoratet/municipal documents already in this knowledge base, for any figure that needs to be relied on as authoritative.
