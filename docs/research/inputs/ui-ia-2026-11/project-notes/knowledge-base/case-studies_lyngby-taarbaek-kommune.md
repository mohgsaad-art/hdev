# Lyngby-Taarbæk Kommune — Fleet Electrification Case Study

**Source file:** `Casehistorier_Fagfolk_templafy.pdf` (compendium; story: "Gode erfaringer med brug af elbiler i hjemmeplejen og intern service")
**Category:** Case study — fleet electrification (municipal elderly care & internal service)
**Original language:** Danish (translated to English below; proper nouns kept in Danish)
**One-line summary:** Lyngby-Taarbæk Kommune's elderly care and internal service staff use 8 Renault Zoe/Kangoo EVs with positive feedback across nurses, care assistants and office attendants, and plans to grow the electric share to 30% of the home-care fleet.

## Company Profile
- **Organization:** Lyngby-Taarbæk Kommune — ældrepleje (elderly care) and intern service (internal service/office attendants)
- **Sector:** Municipal elderly care and internal administrative service
- **Fleet:** 7 Renault Zoe and Renault Kangoo vehicles (per "Fakta" box) / 8 EVs referenced in body text ("otte elbiler") — both figures given verbatim, not reconciled in source
- **Annual driving need:** 17,000 km/year
- **Person(s) quoted:** Christian Werge Vaarby — bilansvarlig (vehicle-responsible officer)

## Electrification Timeline
- As part of the municipality's climate strategy, 8 EVs were purchased for elderly-care staff and internal-service office attendants.
- No specific start year given in this story.
- Municipality has developed a strategy for establishing charging facilities at its care centers (pleje-centre), ensuring the necessary number of chargers is always available, including guest chargers as part of the overall infrastructure plan.
- Municipality is trialing hybrid vehicles on an experimental basis at locations where charging stations cannot currently be installed.
- Planned expansion: 6 more EVs to be acquired "til sommer" (by summer), after which EVs will make up 30% of home care's total vehicle count.

## Financial Figures
- No specific DKK cost or savings figures, and no CO2 tonnage figure, are stated in this source.
- Municipal vehicles are purchased with a buy-back clause (tilbagekøbsklausul), guaranteeing a fixed resale price from the dealer after 5 years. Christian Werge Vaarby notes: "Vi har en fornuftig totaløkonomi på bilerne, og driften ser ud til at blive bedre end forventet" (We have a sensible total economy on the vehicles, and operations look set to be better than expected) — qualitative, no numeric figure given.

## Operational Notes
- Vehicles are fully equipped, matching conventional vehicles on safety and comfort features, including heated seats.
- Staff report the setup works well and consistently meets operational requirements: "Elbilerne kører i faste rammer, hvor vi ved at de kan løfte opgaven – at det hænger sammen. Og det gør det."
- A specific improvement area identified: staff should get better at remembering to use the pre-heating (forvarme) function and get more comfortable/familiar with it.
- Charging is currently limited to day/evening shift use, not overnight, but the plan to move to 22 kW chargers is intended to improve utilization further.

(Going forward we need 22 kW charging boxes so we can get even better utilization of the EVs, because they do the job and users are really happy to drive electric.)

## Future Plans
- Acquire 6 more EVs "til sommer" (by summer), bringing electric share of home care's fleet to 30%.
- Upgrade from 11 kW to 22 kW charging boxes for better fleet utilization, including enabling overnight/expanded use.
- Continued gradual phase-in of EVs municipality-wide, with hybrid vehicles used as an interim/experimental option at sites lacking charging infrastructure.
