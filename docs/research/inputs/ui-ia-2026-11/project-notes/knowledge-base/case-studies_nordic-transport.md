# Nordic Transport A/S — Fleet Electrification Case Study

**Source file:** `CASE_Nordic_FINAL.pdf`
**Category:** Case study — fleet electrification
**Original language:** Danish (translated to English below; proper nouns kept in Danish)
**One-line summary:** A Danish transport/haulage company founded on one van in 2014 is going "all-in" on electric vans, targeting 100% CO2-neutral operations within a few years.

## Company Profile
- **Company:** Nordic Transport A/S
- **Industry/sector:** Road transport / haulage (parcel and goods distribution, "vognmandsforretning")
- **Fleet size:** Over 40 vehicles (biler), 60 employees ("medarbejdere")
- **Locations/depots:** Avedøre (head office, Hvidovre Kommune), Randers, Ringe
- **Person(s) quoted:** Mohammad Al-Karagholi — founder/owner (bought his first van in 2014); an unnamed driver ("chauffør") quoted about driving an electric van
- **Publication year of case:** 2021

## Electrification Timeline
- **2014:** Mohammad Al-Karagholi buys his first van (not stated as electric at this point — this marks the start of the company).
- **2018:** The journey toward being CO2-neutral begins, triggered by a customer request asking for goods to be delivered using an electric van ("elvarebil"). This is described as the "starting shot" ("startskuddet").
- **First electric van:** Bought "a few years ago" (relative to 2021) to gain experience. Max range 120 km, which created route-planning challenges that the company said it quickly got under control.
- **Current phase (as of 2021):** Nordic Transport is in the process of purchasing a series of 3.5-tonne electric vans with a range increased to 350 km.
- **2022 (planned):** All routes in Copenhagen to be handled with electric vans only; the company states it will phase out diesel van driving in its own self-declared "city zones" (byzoner) ahead of any political mandate, expanding electric vans to other major Danish cities as the fleet is replaced.
- **Stated long-term goal:** Become 100% CO2-neutral "within a few years" (few years from 2021).

## Operational Notes
- Each route has been individually optimized and planned; the company had to build its own distribution system from scratch to accommodate limited EV range.
- Charging strategy: fast depot charging (6 vehicles/45 min) planned for daytime turnaround, plus 11 kW overnight chargers for vans that "sleep over" at HQ.
- Self-imposed low-emission "city zones": Nordic Transport is phasing out diesel vans in major cities on its own initiative, ahead of national/city regulation.
- No V2X or smart-charging/grid-management details mentioned in source.

## Quotes
- Mohammad Al-Karagholi: "Vi købte den første elvarebil for nogle år siden for at gøre os nogle erfaringer. Med en max rækkevidde på 120 km gav det nogle planlægningsmæssige udfordringer, men det fik vi nu hurtigt styr på." (We bought the first electric van some years ago to gain experience. With a max range of 120 km it created some planning challenges, but we quickly got that under control.)
- Mohammad Al-Karagholi, on the new 3.5t EVs with 350 km range: "Så begynder det at ligne noget." (Now it's starting to look like something.)
- Mohammad Al-Karagholi: "Vi indfører vores egne byzoner og udfaser al kørsel med dieselvarebiler i storbyerne." (We are introducing our own city zones and phasing out all diesel van driving in the big cities.)
- Customer feedback (paraphrased by Mohammad): customers appreciate no longer being woken by engine noise during night deliveries.
- An unnamed driver: "En vagt med elbil er en dejlig dag – man har slet ikke hovedpine, når man kommer hjem." (A shift with an electric van is a lovely day — you don't get a headache at all when you get home.)
- Mohammad Al-Karagholi, on being a first mover: "Om 5-10 år bliver det et krav, at transportvirksomheder kan levere bæredygtig transport. Og jeg vil hellere være First Mover end at sidde på perronen og se, at toget er kørt. Det koster måske nogle lærepenge her og nu, men jeg er sikker på, at vi kommer til at stå stærkere i fremtiden." (In 5-10 years it will be a requirement that transport companies can deliver sustainable transport. And I'd rather be a First Mover than sit on the platform watching the train leave. It may cost some tuition money now, but I'm sure we'll be stronger in the future.)

## Future Plans
- All Copenhagen routes handled by electric vans by 2022.
- Gradual roll-out of electric vans to other major Danish cities as the fleet is replaced.
- Target: 100% CO2-neutral within a few years (from 2021 baseline).
- Own charging station at Avedøre HQ to also be made available to other local companies.
