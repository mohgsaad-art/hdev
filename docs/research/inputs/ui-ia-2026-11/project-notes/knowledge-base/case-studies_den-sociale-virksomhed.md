# Den Sociale Virksomhed — Fleet Electrification Case Study

**Source file:** `Casehistorier_Fagfolk_templafy.pdf` (compendium; story: "Flere elbiler i takt med udviklingen hos Den Sociale Virksomhed")
**Category:** Case study — fleet electrification (regional social services)
**Original language:** Danish (translated to English below; proper nouns kept in Danish)
**One-line summary:** Den Sociale Virksomhed, part of Region Hovedstaden and operating 20 specialized social/psychiatric facilities, runs 4 EVs out of a fleet of 70+ vehicles, with a regional "rejsehold" (traveling task force) analyzing further electrification potential.

## Company Profile
- **Organization:** Den Sociale Virksomhed (part of Region Hovedstaden)
- **Sector:** Social and social-psychiatric services — operates 20 highly specialized facilities: residential and day facilities, special education, activity and employment services, treatment and rehabilitation, hostels/shelters (herberger), crisis centers, and secure units for young people
- **Fleet size:** More than 70 vehicles total, of which 4 are electric
- **Example location cited:** Nordsjællands Misbrugscenter (North Zealand Addiction Center), where a Renault Zoe is pictured/referenced
- **Person(s) quoted:** Carsten Falk — economist (økonom), lead ("tovholder") on the acquisition of the first EVs

## Electrification Timeline
- No specific start date given for the first 4 EVs.
- At time of writing, the regional EV secretariat's affiliated traveling task force ("rejsehold") is actively mapping and analyzing the 70+-vehicle fleet to identify further electrification potential beyond the 4 EVs already acquired.
- Adoption approach: EVs are acquired when a specific task can be handled by one ("Kan den det, så anskaffes en elbil"); if not, vehicles may be swapped between locations so an EV can be used elsewhere instead.

## Financial Figures
- No specific DKK or CO2 tonnage figures are stated in this source.
- Noted driver: increased interest in using pool cars (incl. EVs) instead of employees' own private cars, following a change that reduced the mileage reimbursement rate for using a personal car for work travel ("efter at befordring i egen bil ikke længere takseres til høj takst").

## Operational Notes
- Vehicle acquisition follows "almindelig sund fornuft" (ordinary common sense) combined with a concrete assessment of task suitability.
- New users need some introduction/adjustment time: cables and charging are new to everyone, and automatic transmission is new for many — described as requiring "lidt introduktion og tilvænning" (a bit of introduction and habituation).
- Staff sentiment: some initial skepticism resolved quickly once staff actually tried driving electric.

## Quotes
- Carsten Falk: "Vi følger udviklingen i samarbejde med det regionale elbilsekretariat og i takt med, at flere af vores opgaver kan løses med elbiler, øges vores andel tilsvarende." (We follow developments in collaboration with the regional EV secretariat, and as more of our tasks can be solved with EVs, our share increases correspondingly.)
- Carsten Falk: "Vores erfaringer er, at elbiler i dag er et reelt alternativ til fossil transport og at elbiler godt kan dække vores behov." (Our experience is that EVs today are a real alternative to fossil transport and that EVs can indeed cover our needs.)
- Carsten Falk: "Vores medarbejdere er glade for elbilerne. Der var enkelte som var lidt skeptiske i starten, men det er lige indtil de prøver og oplever hvor let og lige til, det er at køre elektrisk. Kabler og opladning er nyt for alle og automatgear er også nyt for mange, så elbiler kræver lidt introduktion og tilvænning." (Our employees are happy with the EVs. There were a few who were a bit skeptical at first, but that's only until they try it and experience how easy and straightforward it is to drive electric. Cables and charging are new to everyone and automatic transmission is also new for many, so EVs require a bit of introduction and habituation.)

## Future Plans
- Electric share of the fleet expected to grow in proportion to how many tasks can be matched to EVs, guided by ongoing analysis from the regional EV secretariat's traveling task force.
