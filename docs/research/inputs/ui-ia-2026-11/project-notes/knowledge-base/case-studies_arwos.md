# Arwos — Fleet Electrification Case Study

**Source file:** `Casehistorier_Fagfolk_templafy.pdf` (compendium; story: "Elbilen går rent ind hos Arwos")
**Category:** Case study — EV demonstration/awareness event (municipal utility)
**Original language:** Danish (translated to English below; proper nouns kept in Danish)
**One-line summary:** Aabenraa municipal utility Arwos hosted the "Mød elbilen" (Meet the EV) demonstration event, letting around 100 employees test-drive 4 modern EVs, sparking strong internal interest and reinforcing its existing 2-EV service-vehicle fleet.

## Company Profile
- **Organization:** Arwos — Aabenraa Kommunes forsyningsselskab (Aabenraa Municipality's utility company), handling waste, recycling, water, wastewater and landfill/deponi ("Navnet Arwos betyder 'Vores arv'" — the name Arwos means "our heritage")
- **Industry/sector:** Municipal utilities (affald, genbrug, vand, spildevand, deponi)
- **Employees:** ca. 100 (all offered the chance to try the demo EVs, "fra den ph.d.-studerende til ufaglærte og det administrative personale")
- **Existing EV fleet (service vehicles):** 2 electric vehicles already in use among service vehicles at time of writing
- **Location:** Aabenraa Kommune (a geographically large municipality, per source)
- **Person(s) quoted:** Konny Madsen — Administrationschef (Administration Manager)

## Electrification Timeline
- Arwos learned about Region Hovedstaden's "Mød elbilen" demonstration project via an employee and immediately offered all staff the chance to test 4 different EVs.
- Testing took place in the weeks leading up to Christmas.
- At the time of the demo, Arwos already had 2 EVs among its service vehicles ("Blandt virksomhedens servicebiler... findes allerede to elbiler, så den grønne omstilling af bilflåden er påbegyndt").
- "Mød elbilen" (Meet the EV) is described as a demonstration project targeted at private companies, aiming to accelerate private-sector EV adoption via positive, hands-on exposure. In total 25 companies were given the opportunity to test 4 modern EVs with charging boxes for 14 days, including instruction, help-desk, and recommendations for their ongoing transition process. Funded by Energistyrelsen under the Ministry of Climate, Energy and Building.

## Operational Notes
- Most vehicles at Arwos cover long distances given Aabenraa Kommune's large geographic size; EVs are deployed into operations "where it is possible" ("Elbiler indsættes i driften, hvor det er muligt").
- Employee reception: driving experiences described as consistently positive — "gode at køre i, faktisk sjove, men også lidt underlige, for de kører helt uden lyd" (nice to drive, actually fun, but also a bit strange, since they run completely silently).
- Several employees, after the demo, began calculating whether switching their own petrol/diesel car to electric would make financial sense; interest reached the leadership/director level too, with particular interest in Tesla noted.
- Company procedure: whenever new vehicles are to be acquired, the most environmentally sound option is always considered — the Mød elbilen initiative reinforced this awareness both for the company and for employees personally.

## Quotes
- Konny Madsen (Administrationschef): "Som miljøvirksomhed har vi en fast procedure når der skal anskaffes nye biler og vi overvejer altid den mest miljørigtige løsning. Et initiativ som Mød elbilen er med til at bevidstgøre miljøhensynet både for os som virksomhed og for medarbejderne, når de skal købe bil." (As an environmental company we have a fixed procedure whenever new vehicles are to be acquired, and we always consider the most environmentally sound solution. An initiative like Mød elbilen helps raise environmental awareness both for us as a company and for employees when they're buying a car.)
- Konny Madsen: "I takt med udviklingen og et stadig større udbud af elbiler, så vil vi helt sikkert se flere elbiler hos os." (As development continues and the range of EVs keeps growing, we will certainly see more EVs at our company.)
