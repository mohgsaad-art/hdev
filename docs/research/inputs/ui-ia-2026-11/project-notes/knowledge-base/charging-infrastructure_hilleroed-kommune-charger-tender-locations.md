**One-line summary:** An appendix ("Bilag 1, Lokalitetsoversigt" — Location Overview) listing 40 specific municipal-building parking-lot sites in Hillerød Kommune, grouped into 5 tender sub-agreements ("Delaftaler") of 8 sites each, with detailed per-site technical/traffic data for a charge-point installation tender.

## Administrative / Issuing Body
- Site list includes **Hillerød Rådhus, Trollesminde Alle 27, 3400** (site #24) — matching the address of Hillerød Kommune's town hall as given in the companion strategy document (`Strategi-for-lade-standere-og-emissionsfrie-koeretoejer_Hilleroed-Kommune.pdf`), strongly indicating this is a **Hillerød Kommune** tender appendix, though the document itself does not carry an explicit municipality letterhead in the extracted text. Treated here as a companion/appendix document to Hillerød Kommune's charging strategy, based on this address match and shared place names (Hillerød, Gørløse, Skævinge, Uvelse, Nødebo, Favrholm, Ny Hammersholt — all within Hillerød Kommune's municipal area).
- **Document type:** "Lokalitets ID" / "Byområder" (Location ID / Urban areas) listing, structured as a tender-appendix site catalogue split into 5 "Delaftaler" (sub-agreements/lots), 8 sites each, totaling **40 sites**.

## Data Fields Used Per Site (structural note — Claude's own framing)
Each site entry in the source consistently records: Lokalitetsnummer (site number), Sted (place name), Adresse (address), Ladeudtag/minimum (minimum charge outlets required, with reference to a marked map field not reproducible here), Ladetype & særlige forhold (charger type and special conditions, e.g. "mulighed for at trække ampere fra bygning" = possibility of drawing amperage from the building), Formål (purpose/use of the site), Primære målgruppe (primary target user group), Trafik henover døgnet (traffic pattern over the day, described qualitatively per site's purpose), P-plads tæt på (nearby land uses, e.g. "Beboelse (etage)" = multi-story residential, "Beboelse (åben)" = detached-house residential, "Beboelse (tæt/lav)" = dense-low residential), Trafiktal (ÅDT) (Annual Average Daily Traffic count), Nærmeste snittælling (nearest traffic count cross-section location), and P-plads synlig fra talt vejsnit (whether the parking lot is visible from the counted road section — a rough visibility/exposure proxy).

## Notable Sites (Claude's own highlighting, not from source)
- **Site 39, Favrholm Station** is by far the largest-scale single site: **50 charge outlets** total lot design capacity, up to **800A** drawable from the transformer, **14,044 ÅDT** (highest traffic count in the list) — tied to major nearby development (a new super-hospital opening 2025, a new district under local-plan consultation, a new stadium quarter from 2027).
- **Site 26, Royal Stage/Sundhedshuset/Svømmehal** is notable for already hosting **18 charge points dedicated to the municipality's home-care ("hjemmepleje") vehicle fleet** in a "blue-marked area" — directly matching the home-care EV conversion plan described in the companion Hillerød Kommune strategy document (15 home-care EVs converted in 2022, each assigned its own charger, based at Plejecenter Præsthøjgården per that document — note the specific site name differs slightly between the two documents, "Royal Stage/Sundhedshuset/Svømmehal" here vs. "Sundhedscentret" in the strategy narrative; both are plausibly the same or adjacent municipal health-campus location, not confirmed identical in the extracted text).
- **Site 24, Hillerød Rådhus**, and **Site 3, Hillerød Holmsskolen**, are tied for the second-highest minimum outlet requirement (6 outlets each) among standard sites, both allowing AC and/or DC (hurtiglader).

## Relevance Notes for HDEV/Depot Tool (Claude's own framing, not from source)
- This is a granular, site-level tender specification for **public/municipal-building light-duty EV charging** (schools, daycare centers, care homes, cultural facilities, a train station) — not depot/HD-vehicle charging, but it is a useful real-world example of how a Danish municipality structures a multi-lot charging tender (splitting 40 sites into 5 balanced 8-site "Delaftaler" for competitive bidding) and how site-level technical/traffic data is documented (ÅDT traffic counts, minimum outlet counts, "mulighed for at trække ampere fra bygning" building-amperage options, and transformer capacity notes like the 800A figure at Favrholm Station).
- The Favrholm Station site (50-outlet design capacity, 800A transformer draw) is the closest analog in this batch to depot-scale electrical planning, since it is sized well beyond typical single-building normal-charger installations — useful as a real Danish reference point for what an 800A/multi-outlet public charging hub commitment looks like in practice.
