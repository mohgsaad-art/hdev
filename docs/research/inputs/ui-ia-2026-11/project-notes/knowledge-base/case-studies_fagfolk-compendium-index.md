## About This Document
This PDF is a multi-story compendium (not a single case study). It presents ~22 distinct short case stories about Danish organizations' and individuals' experience adopting electric vehicles (and in a few cases electric tools/scooters). Most stories reference two specific public programs:
- **"Hovedstadsregionens elbilpartnerskab"** (Capital Region's EV Partnership) — a joint-procurement/subsidy program for EVs, supported by Energistyrelsen (the Danish Energy Agency), covering more than 500 EVs across public and private partners.
- **"Mød elbilen" (Meet the EV)** — a demonstration project for private companies, letting 25 companies test 4 modern EVs plus charging boxes for 14 days including instruction/help-desk, funded by Energistyrelsen under the Ministry of Climate, Energy and Building.

Because most stories are undated internally but reference "Better Place" (which went bankrupt ~2013) as a past/legacy technology and describe the described EV partnership programs as current, this compendium is assessed to originate from roughly **2014–2017** — materially earlier than the standalone 2021/2023 CASE PDFs elsewhere in this knowledge base. Where a story overlaps with a later standalone case (e.g. Ishøj Kommune hjemmepleje), both are kept as separate files since the fleet size, dates, and named contacts differ.

## Notes on Scope and Data Quality
- These are short, largely qualitative stories (typically 1-2 pages each); many lack DKK financial figures, exact dates, or CO2 tonnage data. Where a figure is not given, the individual case file states "Not clearly stated in source" rather than estimating.
- Several cases share the same background facts about the "Hovedstadsregionens elbilpartnerskab" or "Mød elbilen" programs — these shared programme facts are repeated verbatim in each relevant case file's Company Profile / Electrification Timeline sections, since the instruction was to make each file self-contained and exhaustive of what appears in its own source pages.
