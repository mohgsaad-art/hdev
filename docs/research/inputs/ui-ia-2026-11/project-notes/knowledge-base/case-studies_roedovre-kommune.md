# Rødovre Kommune (Teknisk Forvaltning) — Fleet Electrification Case Study

**Source file:** `Casehistorier_Fagfolk_templafy.pdf` (compendium; story: "Handlingsplan for omstilling til elbiler påviser økonomisk potentiale")
**Category:** Case study — fleet electrification (municipal technical administration)
**Original language:** Danish (translated to English below; proper nouns kept in Danish)
**One-line summary:** A fleet analysis by the regional EV secretariat's traveling task force found that 10 of Rødovre Kommune's Technical Administration vehicles (28% of that fleet) could favorably be replaced with EVs, leading to 2 Volkswagen e-up! already deployed in municipal cleaning and a concrete action plan for further rollout.

## Company Profile
- **Organization:** Rødovre Kommune — Teknisk Forvaltning (Technical Administration), specifically its municipal cleaning unit (Rødovre Kommunale Rengøring)
- **Sector:** Municipal technical administration / municipal cleaning services
- **Fleet featured:** 2 Volkswagen e-up! (deployed in municipal cleaning)
- **Fleet analyzed:** Vehicles under 3,500 kg in Teknisk Forvaltning
- **Person(s) quoted:** Marianne Holm Andersen — most involved in the fleet analysis (flådeanalysen); Ole Jeppesen — driftsansvarlig (operations manager)

## Electrification Timeline
- Under the climate strategy for the capital region, agreed between KKR Hovedstaden and Region Hovedstaden, Rødovre Kommune had its Teknisk Forvaltning vehicle fleet analyzed for EV potential, in collaboration with the regional EV secretariat's traveling task force (rejsehold).
- The mapping found that 10 of the current vehicles under 3,500 kg could favorably be replaced with EVs — corresponding to 28% of that sub-fleet, which the source notes aligns with the capital region's climate strategy target that at least 25% of the public vehicle fleet in the region be electric or otherwise fossil-free-powered.
- A dedicated "elbilgruppe" (EV group) was established within Teknisk Forvaltning to pave the way for EV adoption.
- The first 2 EVs (Volkswagen e-up!) were acquired and put into service in municipal cleaning.

## Operational Notes
- The analysis and resulting concrete action plan ("konkret handlingsplan") are explicitly intended as an input for further decision-making on introducing more EVs across the municipality; similar analyses are planned for the municipality's other administrative departments.
- The largest remaining potential is flagged in Socialforvaltningen (Social Administration), where home care alone operates 40–50 vehicles — described by Marianne Holm Andersen as offering major potential ("Her er der stort potentiale").
- User feedback on the deployed Volkswagen e-up!s is strongly positive: users like the absence of gears, good acceleration, and high equipment level (heated windshield, remote pre-heating, navigation system showing energy consumption/regeneration).
- The Volkswagen smartphone app lets users compare their own consumption figures against others, which has fostered increased energy awareness and even a degree of friendly competition over who drives most energy-efficiently.

## Future Plans
- Continued work on the action plan within Teknisk Forvaltning, with more EVs expected to follow.
- Similar fleet-electrification analyses planned for the municipality's other administrative departments, with particular attention to the large home-care fleet (40-50 vehicles) in Socialforvaltningen.
