## Section 1: General
The BUT (Terms of Tender and Bid) contains informational content about the tendered concession contract and binding provisions for the bid, evaluation, and award phases. The BUT applies **until contract signing**, after which it lapses.

## Section 2: Overview/Information
Template fields for the contracting authority's identity and contact details, and the statement that the tender concerns the authority granting the right to establish and operate publicly accessible charging stations at various locations to **<one operator/several operators>**, per the **AFI-loven (Lov nr. 412 af 4. april 2022 om infrastruktur for alternative drivmidler til transport)** (Act on infrastructure for alternative fuels for transport). Optionally, the authority may state the tender also fulfills its obligations under the **Ladestanderbekendtgørelsen** (Executive order of 5 March 2020 on preparation for and establishment of charging stations in connection with buildings).

The tender is conducted as a **concession tender below the threshold value** relative to the **Koncessionsdirektivet** (EU Concessions Directive), since the concession's value is below the threshold (calculated per the method in Koncessionsdirektivets artikel 8, stk. 3). This means the concession contract must be awarded based on an announcement per **udbudslovens § 192** and rules issued under **udbudslovens § 191, stk. 2**, per **Bekendtgørelse nr. 1073 af 28. juni 2022** (Executive Order on contracting authorities' agreements on the right to operate publicly accessible charging points).

Private operator(s) generally self-finance establishment, operation, and removal costs of the charging stations. The contracting authority may exceptionally pay operators of publicly accessible charging points if, following a tender or market-conformity check per **udbudslovens § 193**, it is established the agreement cannot otherwise be concluded — per **bekendtgørelse nr. 414 af 4. april 2022** on municipalities' and regions' payment to operators of publicly accessible charging points.

Template fields specify: total minimum number of charging points across a minimum number of locations; number of "puljer" (pools/bundles) of locations being tendered; whether bids must cover all pools or may cover a subset; and whether additional (non-Ladestanderbekendtgørelsen-mandated) locations are included as options.

The tender is run as a process resembling a **restricted tender / negotiated tender / open tender** (mutually exclusive options in the template). Agreements may run for a **maximum of 10 years**, extendable to **15 years** if justified by extraordinary infrastructure investment considerations.

### Price List Guidance
In the Tilbudsliste (Price List), the bidder states its price per location, per sub-agreement, and per option:
- **Negative numbers** represent the lump-sum payment the bidder will make (i.e., the bidder pays the contracting authority) to establish/operate the charging stations for the concession's full duration.
- **Positive numbers** represent the lump-sum payment the bidder requires from the contracting authority to establish/operate the charging stations for the concession's full duration.

## Section 3: Tender

### 3.1 Language
Bids must be submitted in **Danish**, and the tender process conducted in Danish. Technical/descriptive documents may optionally be accepted in **English, Swedish, or Norwegian**. If a negotiated tender is used, negotiations are also conducted in Danish.

### 3.2 Tender Material
The tender material comprises:
- The announcement on **udbud.dk** (the Danish public tender portal)
- This BUT document
- Draft concession contract
- Kravspecifikation (Requirements Specification)
- Tilbudsliste (Price List)
- Response form regarding the Operator's Service Level
- Bilag 1: Overview and location profile sheets, incl. cadastral boundaries
- Bilag 2: Overview of any additional locations, incl. cadastral boundaries
- Bilag 3: Charging infrastructure strategy/plan
- Bilag 4: Working environment document
- Bilag 5: Document on data subjects' rights
- Bilag 6: Template declaration on oath regarding unpaid overdue debt to public authorities
- Bilag 7: Concession calculation

### 3.3 Conditions for Participation
Template placeholder section — to be completed only if relevant, potentially covering exclusion grounds, selection criteria, and/or minimum suitability requirements.

### 3.4 Requirements and Minimum Requirements
**Krav** (Requirements): all requirements in the tender material, which must be met upon concession contract performance. If a bid deviates from a requirement, this is handled as a **forbehold** (reservation/qualification) per section 6.3.

**Mindstekrav** (Minimum requirements): fundamental requirements the contracting authority may not change, either during the tender phase or after contract signing; must be met upon contract performance. **A bid deviating from a minimum requirement must be rejected.** Phrases in the tender material like "shall as a minimum contain…" or "it is a minimum requirement that…" should **not** automatically be read as "mindstekrav" in the formal procurement-law sense. Deviations from ordinary requirements are handled as reservations per section 6.3.

### 3.5 Alternative Bids
Template option: **either** alternative bids are not accepted, **or** alternative bids are accepted (in which case they must meet the tender material's stated minimum requirements).

### 3.6 Award
Template offers a choice between **price as the sole award criterion**, or **best price-quality ratio**. Guidance note: given the many different approaches to the charging-operator role, it may be advisable to weight on more than price alone.

#### 3.6.1 Sub-Criteria (Underkriterier)
Included only if the award criterion is "best price-quality ratio," or as needed for a "costs" criterion; weighting/prioritization can optionally be specified.

Important clarification: competition parameters (in the Operatørens Serviceniveau document) are **not requirements** in the same sense as the Kravspecifikation — a bid is not automatically non-conforming if the operator doesn't answer or meet one or more competition parameters; instead, this is reflected in the qualitative evaluation of the bid. After contract signing, the Operator is obligated to deliver per **both** the Kravspecifikation **and** whatever competition-parameter commitments it made in its response.

If an operator bids on multiple sub-contracts, it may choose to submit one combined Service Level response covering several/all sub-contracts bid on, or a separate response per sub-contract if offering different service levels — the operator should indicate in the Service Level table which sub-contracts its response covers.

**Template sub-criteria structure:**
| Sub-criterion | Description |
|---|---|
| A | Price |
| B | \<Functionality, payment system and data> |
| C | \<Workflows and processes> |
| D | \<nn> (additional, as needed) |

**Sub-criterion B (Functionality, payment system and data)** — evaluated on the extent/level to which the bidder offers high quality regarding charging-station functionality and the associated payment/data-delivery system, assessed against how well the bidder's solution meets competition parameters **B.1–B.8** as set out in the Operatørens Serviceniveau document (which also states what is weighted positively for each parameter).

**Sub-criterion C (Workflows and processes)** — evaluated on the extent/level to which the bidder offers high quality in workflows and processes, assessed against competition parameters **C.1–C.6** as set out in the Operatørens Serviceniveau document.

*Note: this BUT template's references to Underkriterium B (points B.1–B.8) and C (points C.1–C.6) align closely with, though number slightly more items than, the six B-criteria and five C-criteria fully detailed in the companion document `56b8be287f1e4500b2e23f3203039ab4.pdf` ("Operatørens serviceniveau"), suggesting either an updated/expanded criteria set in some tender variants or minor version differences between template documents in this series.*

#### 3.6.2 Evaluation Model
Included only for "best price-quality ratio" awards. Uses a **"price model"** evaluation approach: for each bid, a total evaluation price is calculated as the bid sum, adjusted by a **fictional surcharge or deduction** for each qualitative sub-criterion. At **average quality**, no adjustment is made; **above-average quality** yields a deduction from the bid sum; **below-average quality** yields a surcharge to the bid sum. The total evaluation price is fictional and used only for evaluation purposes — it does not represent the actual contract sum. The concession is awarded to the bidder(s) with the **lowest total evaluation price**. The bid sum used in the evaluation model is a technical evaluation bid sum, where the payment plan is discounted using a **discount rate of 3.5%**, per the Tilbudsliste (Price List).

**Grading scale (0–10) with fictional surcharge/deduction to the bid sum, per sub-criterion (B and C shown with separate multiplier variables X and Y):**

| Grade | Description | Adjustment — Sub-criterion B | Adjustment — Sub-criterion C |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | Excellent bid, best possible fulfillment of the sub-criterion, with no or few insignificant exceptions | −5 × X00,000 | −5 × Y00,000 |
| 9 | Highly satisfactory fulfillment | −4 × X00,000 | −4 × Y00,000 |
| 8 | Very satisfactory fulfillment | −3 × X00,000 | −3 × Y00,000 |
| 7 | Good fulfillment | −2 × X00,000 | −2 × Y00,000 |
| 6 | Slightly above average fulfillment | −X00,000 | −Y00,000 |
| 5 | Average satisfactory fulfillment | 0 | 0 |
| 4 | Slightly below average fulfillment | +X00,000 | +Y00,000 |
| 3 | Less satisfactory fulfillment | +2 × X00,000 | +2 × Y00,000 |
| 2 | Unsatisfactory fulfillment | +3 × X00,000 | +3 × Y00,000 |
| 1 | Very unsatisfactory fulfillment | +4 × X00,000 | +4 × Y00,000 |
| 0 | Bid is only just conforming — sub-criterion just barely met | +5 × X00,000 | +5 × Y00,000 |

(X00,000 and Y00,000 are template placeholders for specific DKK amounts to be set per tender, excluding VAT.)

## Section 4: Negotiations
Included only if relevant (i.e., a negotiated tender procedure is used) and tailored to the specific tender. Template describes an optional phased process: submission of initial bids → negotiation meeting(s) → submission of final bid. Guidance covers: negotiation scope (all aspects of the tender material may be discussed, but not the final bids or fundamental elements); equal treatment of bidders during negotiation; confidentiality of one bidder's proposed solutions from other bidders without consent; no guarantee the same elements are discussed with every bidder (subject to the equal-treatment principle); purpose of negotiation is to ensure the bidder understands the contracting authority's needs and can adjust its offer accordingly; agenda circulated before negotiation meetings; revision of tender material if negotiations reveal aspects of the authority's needs not yet addressed; minutes kept of negotiation proceedings; meeting location; and the authority's right to request follow-up meetings. After negotiations conclude, the authority formally invites final bids with a stated deadline and revised timeline.

## Section 5: Questions on the Tender Material

### 5.1 Questions
Bidders may submit clarifying questions to the contracting authority by a stated deadline (date/time), in **Danish**. Optionally, questions received after the deadline may still be answered unless unreasonably burdensome. Questions (anonymized) and answers are published on an ongoing basis via the tender system, with notification of new publications; all answers given in Danish; publication occurs via corrections/supplementary notices in the tender system, which the contracting authority may issue either on its own initiative or in response to bidder questions.

### 5.2 Information Meeting
Optional section: template for stating meeting location, date, and time; purpose (briefing on the task, opportunity for questions — ideally submitted via the tender system before the meeting, with any questions raised at the meeting later resubmitted via the system); registration requirement (bidders register attendee numbers by a stated date).

Standard required documents:

- Completed version of the Operatørens serviceniveau (Operator's Service Level)
- Declaration of authorization and joint/several liability, if multiple entities bid jointly (bidder may use the attached template or its own)
- Contact information: company name, address, contact person, email address
- \<Other relevant documents>

### 6.2 Appendix Regarding Participation Conditions
Included only if additional appendices must accompany the bid as a condition of participation.

### 6.3 Reservations (Forbehold)
The contracting authority is **obliged to reject** bids containing reservations against fundamental elements of the tender material, and is **entitled to reject** bids with reservations against non-fundamental elements. Reservations against non-fundamental elements will be **capitalized where possible**, using conservative capitalization; if reservations cannot be capitalized, the bid is rejected. Bidders are encouraged to resolve ambiguities/uncertainties by asking questions during the bid phase, to avoid reservations as much as possible. A reservation concerning one sub-contract is assessed only in relation to that sub-contract and will not cause rejection of bids on other sub-contracts.

### 6.4 Submission of the Bid / Initial Bid
Bids (or initial bids) must be submitted **electronically via the tender system**, received by a stated deadline; late submissions are rejected.

## Section 7: Receipt and Evaluation of Bids

### 7.1 Opening of Bids
Bidders do not have access to observe the bid-opening process or obtain information about competitors' bids.

### 7.1.1 Evaluation of Bids
If a bid is found non-conforming (e.g., due to reservations) during evaluation, it is rejected. Conforming bids are evaluated per the stated award criteria and evaluation method. Per data-protection-regulation requirements, if advisors assist in bid evaluation, their firm name(s) must be disclosed: "\<Advisor name(s)> assists as advisor in the bid evaluation."

## Section 8: Award of the Concession Contract
All bidders are notified in writing and simultaneously, via the tender system, of the bid-evaluation outcome.

## Section 9: Bid Validity (Vedståelse af Tilbud)
The [bid/final bid] is binding for **\<2> months** from the bid deadline. Notification of the award decision does **not** release the bidder from its final bid at that point. If the contracting authority needs to extend the bid-validity period, it must obtain bidders' consent.

## Section 10: Freedom-of-Information Access (Aktindsigt)
The contracting authority is subject to Danish administrative-law rules, including freedom-of-information access rules under the **offentligheds- og forvaltningsloven** (Danish Access to Public Administration Files Act and Public Administration Act). Bidders may mark, at the time of bid submission, which elements they consider technical secrets, trade secrets, and/or confidential aspects. The contracting authority is **not bound** by the bidder's markings but will factor them into its assessment of whether and to what extent to grant access requests.
