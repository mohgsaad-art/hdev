## CHAPTER 1 — GENERAL PROVISIONS (Articles 1–5)

### Article 1 — Subject matter
*(as amended by ►M3, Reg (EU) 2022/1379)*

> "This Regulation complements the legal framework for the type-approval of motor vehicles and engines with regard to emissions established by Regulation (EU) No 582/2011 by laying down the rules for issuing licences to operate a simulation tool with a view to determining CO2 emissions and fuel consumption of new vehicles to be sold, registered or put into service in the Union and for operating that simulation tool and declaring the CO2 emissions and fuel consumption values thus determined."

### Article 2 — Scope
*(as amended by ►M3, Reg (EU) 2022/1379)*

1. Subject to Article 4, second paragraph, this Regulation applies to **medium lorries, heavy lorries and heavy buses.**
2. In the case of multi-stage type-approvals or individual approvals of medium and heavy lorries, this Regulation applies to **base lorries**. In the case of heavy buses, this Regulation applies to **primary vehicles, interim vehicles and to complete vehicles or completed vehicles**.
3. This Regulation does **not apply** to off-road vehicles, special purpose vehicles and off-road special purpose vehicles as defined, respectively, in Part A, points 2.1, 2.2 and 2.3 of Annex I to Regulation (EU) 2018/858.

### Article 4 — Vehicle groups
*(►B baseline text)*

> "For the purpose of this Regulation, motor vehicles shall be classified in vehicle groups in accordance with Annex I, Tables 1 to 6."

**Exclusion clause (second paragraph):** Articles 5 to 23 **do not apply** to:
- heavy lorries of **vehicle groups 6, 7, 8, 13, 14, 15, 17, 18 and 19** (Table 1 of Annex I);
- medium lorries of **vehicle groups 51, 52, 55 and 56** (Table 2 of Annex I); and
- any vehicle with a **driven front axle** in vehicle groups **11, 12 and 16** (Table 1 of Annex I).

This is a materially important exclusion for anyone sizing/simulating a fleet: 4×4 configurations and several niche/off-road-oriented lorry groups are fully carved out of the licensing/certification/conformity machinery (Articles 5–23), even though they are still "classified" under Annex I.

### Article 5 — Electronic tools
*(as amended by ►M1 and ►M3)*

1. *(►M3)* The Commission shall provide **free of charge** the following electronic tools as downloadable, executable software:
   (a) a **simulation tool**;
   (b) **pre-processing tools**;
   (c) a **hashing tool**.
   The Commission maintains these tools and provides modifications/updates.
2. *(►M3)* The Commission makes these tools available through a **publicly available dedicated electronic distribution platform**.
3. *(►M1, further amended ►M3)* "The simulation tool shall be used for the purposes of determining CO2 emissions and fuel consumption of new vehicles." The simulation tool is designed to operate on **input information** per Annex III and **input data** per Article 12(1).
4. *(►B)* The **pre-processing tools** are used for verification/compilation of testing results and additional calculations relating to CO2/fuel-consumption-related properties of certain components/STUs/systems, converting them into a format usable by the simulation tool. Used by the manufacturer after performing the tests referred to in **point 4 of Annex V** (engines) and **point 3 of Annex VIII** (air-drag).
5. *(►M3)* The **hashing tool** establishes an unequivocal association between (i) the certified CO2/fuel-consumption-related properties of a component/STU/system and its certification document, and (ii) a vehicle and its manufacturer's records file, vehicle information file and customer information file (per Annex IV).

## CHAPTER 2 — LICENCE TO OPERATE THE SIMULATION TOOL FOR THE PURPOSES OF TYPE-APPROVAL WITH REGARD TO EMISSIONS (Articles 6–8)

*(Chapter title as amended by ►M3; originally titled differently in ►B.)*

### Article 6 — Application for a licence to operate the simulation tool
*(mixed ►B / ►M3)*

1. *(►M3)* The **vehicle manufacturer** shall submit to the approval authority an application for a **licence** to operate the simulation tool for an **application case**, with a view to determining CO2 emissions and fuel consumption of new vehicles belonging to one or more vehicle groups. **An individual licence applies to only a single application case.** The application must be accompanied by an adequate description of the processes set up by the vehicle manufacturer for operating the simulation tool for that application case, per **point (1) of Annex II**.
2. *(►B)* The application takes the form of an **information document** drawn up per the model in **Appendix 1 to Annex II**.
3. *(►B)* The application must be accompanied by: (a) an adequate description of the processes set up by the manufacturer for determining CO2/fuel consumption for all vehicle groups concerned (point 1 of Annex II); and (b) the **assessment report** drafted by the approval authority after an assessment per **point 2 of Annex II**.
4. *(►M3)* **Timing requirement:** the vehicle manufacturer must submit the licence application **at the latest together with**:
   - the application for EC type-approval of a vehicle with an approved engine system with regard to emissions pursuant to Article 7 of Regulation (EU) No 582/2011;
   - the application for EC type-approval of a vehicle with regard to emissions pursuant to Article 9 of that Regulation;
   - the application for whole-vehicle type-approval pursuant to Regulation (EU) 2018/858; **or**
   - the application for national individual vehicle approval.

   For **pure electric vehicles**, the approval of the electric engine system / EC type-approval referenced above is **limited to the measurement of net engine power** in accordance with **Annex XIV to Regulation (EU) No 582/2011**.

   The licence application must concern the **application case which includes the type of vehicle** covered by the EU type-approval application.

### Article 7 — Administrative provisions for the granting of the licence
*(mixed ►B / ►M3)*

1. *(►M3)* The approval authority **shall grant the licence** if the vehicle manufacturer submits an application per Article 6 and proves that the requirements of **Annex II** are met for the application case concerned.
2. *(►B)* The licence is issued per the model in **Appendix 2 to Annex II**.

### Article 8 — Subsequent changes to the processes set up for determining CO2 emissions and fuel consumption of vehicles
*(mixed ►B / ►M3; paragraph 1 was deleted by ►M3 — marked "▼M3 __________")*

- **Paragraph 1:** deleted (shown in the consolidated text only as "▼M3 __________" — i.e. ►M3 removed this paragraph without substituting text; the original ►B paragraph 1 content is not shown in this consolidated text).
2. *(►M3)* The vehicle manufacturer shall **apply for an extension of the licence** in accordance with Article 6(1), (2) and (3).
3. *(►M3)* After obtaining the licence, the vehicle manufacturer shall **notify the approval authority without delay** of any changes to its processes for the application case covered by the licence that **may affect the accuracy, reliability and stability** of those processes.
4. *(►B)* Upon receiving that notification, the approval authority informs the vehicle manufacturer whether: (i) the changed processes remain covered by the licence; (ii) the licence must be extended per paragraphs 1–2; or (iii) a new licence must be applied for per Article 6.
5. *(►B)* If the changes are **not** covered by the licence, the manufacturer must, **within one month** of receiving that information, apply for an extension or a new licence. **Failure to apply within that deadline, or rejection of the application, results in withdrawal of the licence.**

## CHAPTER 3 — OPERATION OF THE SIMULATION TOOL WITH A VIEW TO DETERMINING THE CO2 EMISSIONS AND FUEL CONSUMPTION FOR THE PURPOSES OF REGISTRATION, SALE AND ENTRY INTO SERVICE OF NEW VEHICLES (Articles 9–11)

### Article 9 — Obligation to determine and declare CO2 emissions and fuel consumption of new vehicles
*(mixed ►B / ►M3)*

1. *(►M3)* A vehicle manufacturer **shall determine** the CO2 emissions and fuel consumption of **each new vehicle** — with the exception of new vehicles using vehicle technologies listed in **Appendix 1 to Annex III** — to be sold, registered or put into service in the Union, using the **latest available version** of the simulation tool (Article 5(3)). For **heavy buses**, the vehicle manufacturer or interim manufacturer uses the method in **Annex I, point (2)**.

   For vehicle technologies listed in **Appendix 1 to Annex III**, the vehicle manufacturer or interim manufacturer determines **only** the input parameters specified for those vehicles in the models of **Table 5 of Annex III**, using the latest simulation-tool version.

   A vehicle manufacturer may operate the simulation tool **only if in possession of a licence** granted for the application case concerned (Article 7). An **interim manufacturer operates the simulation tool under the licence of a vehicle manufacturer** (i.e. interim manufacturers do not need their own licence).

2. *(►B, amended ►M3)* The vehicle manufacturer records the simulation results in the **manufacturer's records file** (model in **Part I of Annex IV**). **Subsequent changes to the manufacturer's records file are prohibited**, except in the cases referred to in the second subparagraph of Article 21(3) and in Article 23(6).

   *(►M3)* Vehicle manufacturers of heavy buses **additionally** record the results in the **vehicle information file**. **Interim manufacturers of heavy buses record the vehicle information file.**

3. *(►M3)* Cryptographic-hash obligations:
   - Vehicle manufacturer of **medium/heavy lorries**: hashes of the manufacturer's records file **and** the customer information file.
   - **Primary vehicle manufacturer** (heavy buses): hashes of the manufacturer's records file **and** the vehicle information file.
   - **Interim manufacturer** (heavy buses): hash of the vehicle information file.
   - Vehicle manufacturer of **complete/completed heavy buses**: hashes of the manufacturer's records file, the customer information file, **and** the vehicle information file.

4. *(►B, amended ►M3)* "Lorries and complete vehicles or completed vehicles that are heavy buses to be registered, sold or to enter into service shall be accompanied by the customer information file" (model in **Part II of Annex IV**), which must include an **imprint of the cryptographic hash** of the manufacturer's records file (per paragraph 3).

   *(►M3)* Vehicle manufacturers of heavy buses must **make the vehicle information file available to the manufacturer of a subsequent step** in the manufacturing chain.

5. *(►B)* Every vehicle accompanied by a **certificate of conformity**, or (for Article-45-of-2018/858 individually approved vehicles) an **individual vehicle approval certificate**, must have that certificate include an **imprint of the cryptographic hashes** referred to in paragraph 3.

6. *(►B)* Per **point 11 of Annex III**, a manufacturer **may transfer results of the simulation tool to other vehicles** (result-transfer / carry-over provision).

### Article 10 — Modifications, updates and malfunction of the electronic tools
*(mixed ►B / ►M3)*

1. *(►B)* On modification/update of the simulation tool, the vehicle manufacturer **shall start using** the modified/updated tool **no later than 3 months** after the modification/update was made available on the dedicated electronic distribution platform.
2. *(►B)* If CO2/fuel consumption **cannot be determined** per Article 9(1) due to a **malfunction of the simulation tool**, the vehicle manufacturer **shall notify the Commission without delay**, via the dedicated electronic distribution platform.
3. *(►B, extended by ►M3)* In that malfunction case, the vehicle manufacturer must perform the simulation **not later than 7 calendar days** after the date in point 1 [of the notification]. Until then, the Article 9 obligations for the affected vehicles are **suspended**.

   *(►M3)* Additional heavy-bus-chain rule: where a malfunction occurs at a manufacturing step **prior to** the complete/completed step, the obligation under Article 9(1) to operate the simulation tool at **subsequent** steps is **postponed for a maximum of 14 calendar days** after the date the previous-step manufacturer made the vehicle information file available to the next manufacturer.

### Article 11 — Accessibility of the simulation tool inputs and output information
*(mixed ►B / ►M3)*

1. *(►M3)* The manufacturer's records file, the vehicle information file, and the certificates on CO2/fuel-consumption-related properties of components/systems/STUs must be **stored by the vehicle manufacturer for at least 20 years** after production of the vehicle, and made available on request to the approval authority and the Commission.
2. *(►M3)* On request by an authorised entity of a Member State or the Commission, the vehicle manufacturer provides the manufacturer's records file or the vehicle information file **within 15 working days**.
3. *(►B)* On the same type of request, the approval authority which granted the licence (Article 7) or certified the CO2/fuel-consumption-related properties of a component/STU/system (Article 17) provides the relevant **information document** (Article 6(2) or Article 16(2)) **within 15 working days**.

## CHAPTER 4 — CO2 EMISSIONS AND FUEL CONSUMPTION RELATED PROPERTIES OF COMPONENTS, SEPARATE TECHNICAL UNITS AND SYSTEMS (Articles 12–19)

### Article 12 — Components, separate technical units and systems relevant for the purposes of determining CO2 emissions and fuel consumption
*(mixed ►B / ►M3 / ►M4)*

1. The simulation tool input data (Article 5(3)) include CO2/fuel-consumption-related-property information for:
   (a) engines;
   (b) transmissions;
   (c) torque converters;
   (d) other torque transferring components;
   (e) additional driveline components;
   (f) axles;
   (g) *(►M3)* air drag;
   (h) auxiliaries;
   (i) tyres;
   (j) *(►M3)* electric powertrain components;
   (k) *(►M4)* wheel ends.

   (Note: this list — (a) through (k) — traces the exact set of "certifiable" CO2/fuel-consumption-relevant component categories in VECTO; **wheel ends**, item (k), is the newest addition, introduced by ►M4/2025.)

2. The properties of components/STUs/systems under (b)–(g), (i), (j) and (k) are based **either** on:
   - values determined per Article 14 and **certified** per Article 17 ("**certified values**"); **or**, absent certified values,
   - **standard values** determined per Article 13.

3. *(►B)* Engine properties are based on values determined per **Article 14** and certified per **Article 17** (engines have **no standard-value fallback** — certification is mandatory).

4. *(►M3)* Auxiliaries' properties are based on **generic values** as implemented in the simulation tool, allocated to a vehicle based on input information determined per **Annex IX**.

5. *(►B)* For a **base lorry**, air-drag properties (paragraph 1(g)) that cannot be determined for the base lorry use **standard values**. For auxiliaries (paragraph 1(h)), the vehicle manufacturer must select **the technology with the highest power losses**.

6. *(►B)* For vehicles exempted from Article 9(1) (determination obligation), the simulation tool input data includes the information set out in **Table 5 of Annex III**.

7. *(►B)* Where a vehicle is registered/sold/put into service with **both** a complete set of snow tyres and a complete set of standard tyres, the manufacturer may choose which tyre set to use for the CO2 determination. For **heavy buses**: as long as the tyres used in the **primary vehicle simulation** remain with the vehicle at registration/sale/service, adding tyre sets afterward does **not** trigger a new primary-vehicle simulation (Annex I, point 2).

### Article 15 — Family concept regarding components, separate technical units and systems using certified values
*(mixed ►B / ►M3 / ►M4)*

1. *(►M4, updating list)* Subject to paragraphs 3–6, certified values for a **parent** component/STU/system are valid, **without further testing**, for all family members per the family definitions in:
   - Appendix 6 to Annex VI (transmissions, torque converters, OTTC, ADC);
   - Appendix 4 to Annex VII (axles);
   - Appendix 5 to Annex VIII (air drag);
   - *(►M3)* Appendix 3 to Annex V (engines) — certified values for engine-family members derived per points 4, 5 and 6 of Annex V;
   - *(►M3)* Appendix 13 to Annex Xb (electric machine systems / integrated electric powertrain components) — certified values for family members derived per point 4 of Annex Xb;
   - *(►M4)* Annex VIIa (wheel ends).

2. *(►M3)* Engines: certified values for family members derived per points 4, 5 and 6 of Annex V. **Tyres: a family consists of one tyre type only** (i.e., no tyre "family" grouping/generalization — each tyre type must be individually certified). Electric machine systems: certified values for family members derived per point 4 of Annex Xb.

3. *(►B)* The parent component/STU/system's CO2/fuel-consumption-related properties **shall not be better than** the properties of any member of the same family (i.e., the parent must represent the **worst case** of the family).

4. *(►B)* The manufacturer must provide the approval authority with evidence that the parent component/STU/system **fully represents** the family. If, during testing under Article 16(3) second subparagraph, the approval authority determines the selected parent does **not** fully represent the family, the approval authority may select, test, and designate an **alternative reference component/STU/system** as the new parent.

5. *(►B)* On the manufacturer's request and subject to approval authority agreement, the CO2/fuel-consumption-related properties of a **specific** (non-parent) component/STU/system may be indicated in the family certificate; such properties must be determined per Article 14.

6. *(►B)* If that specific component's/STU's/system's properties (per paragraph 5) lead to **higher** CO2/fuel-consumption values than the existing parent's, the manufacturer must: (a) exclude it from the existing family, assign it to a **new family**, and designate it the new parent; **or** (b) apply for an **extension of the certification** under Article 18.

### Article 16 — Application for a certification of the CO2 emissions and fuel consumption related properties of components, separate technical units or systems
*(mixed ►B / ►M3 / ►M4)*

1. *(►M3)* The certification application is submitted to the **approval authority**.
2. *(►B, list extended by ►M3/►M4)* The application takes the form of an information document per the model in:
   - Appendix 2 to Annex V (engines);
   - Appendix 2 to Annex VI (transmissions);
   - Appendix 3 to Annex VI (torque converters);
   - Appendix 4 to Annex VI (OTTC);
   - Appendix 5 to Annex VI (ADC);
   - Appendix 2 to Annex VII (axles);
   - Appendix 2 to Annex VIII (air drag);
   - Appendix 2 to Annex X (tyres);
   - *(►M3)* Appendices 2 to 6 to Annex Xb (electric powertrain components);
   - *(►M4)* Appendix 2 to Annex VIIa (wheel ends).
3. *(►M3)* The application must be accompanied by an explanation of the **elements of design** that have a **non-negligible effect** on the CO2/fuel-consumption-related properties, plus *(►B)* the relevant test reports issued by an approval authority, test results, and a **statement of compliance** issued by an approval authority per point 2 of Annex IV to Regulation (EU) 2018/858.

### Article 17 — Administrative provisions for the certification of CO2 emissions and fuel consumption related properties of components, separate technical units and systems
*(mixed ►B / ►M3 / ►M4)*

1. *(►M3)* If all applicable requirements are met, the approval authority **shall certify** the values.
2. *(►B, list extended by ►M3/►M4)* The approval authority issues a **certificate** using the model in:
   - Appendix 1 to Annex V (engines);
   - Appendix 1 to Annex VI (transmissions, torque converters, OTTC, ADC);
   - Appendix 1 to Annex VII (axles);
   - Appendix 1 to Annex VIII (air drag);
   - Appendix 1 to Annex X (tyres);
   - *(►M3)* Appendix 1 to Annex Xb (electric powertrain components);
   - *(►M4)* Appendix 1 to Annex VIIa (wheel ends).
3. *(►B, list extended by ►M3)* The approval authority grants a **certification number** per the numbering systems in:
   - Appendix 6 to Annex V (engines);
   - Appendix 7 to Annex VI (transmissions, torque converters, OTTC, ADC);
   - Appendix 5 to Annex VII (axles);
   - Appendix 8 to Annex VIII (air drag);
   - Appendix 1 to Annex X (tyres);
   - *(►M3)* Appendix 14 to Annex Xb (electric powertrain components).

   The approval authority **shall not assign the same number** to another component/STU/system (or family). **The certification number is used as the identifier of the test report.**
4. *(►M3)* The approval authority creates a **cryptographic hash** of the test-results file (comprising the certification number) using the hashing tool (Article 5(5)), done **immediately after the test results are produced**. It imprints that hash **and** the certification number on the certificate.

### Article 18 — Extension to include a new component, separate technical unit or system into a component family, separate technical unit family or system family
*(mixed ►B / ►M3 / ►M4)*

1. At the manufacturer's request and upon approval authority approval, a new component/STU/system may be included in an existing certified family if it meets the family-definition criteria in:
   - *(►M3)* Appendix 3 to Annex V (engines, taking into account Article 15(2));
   - *(►B)* Appendix 6 to Annex VI (transmissions, torque converters, OTTC, ADC);
   - *(►B)* Appendix 4 to Annex VII (axles);
   - *(►B)* Appendix 5 to Annex VIII (air drag);
   - *(►M3)* Appendix 13 to Annex Xb (electric machine systems / integrated electric powertrain components, taking into account Article 15(2));
   - *(►M4)* Annex VIIa (wheel ends).

   The approval authority issues a **revised certificate denoted by an extension number**. The manufacturer must modify the Article 16(2) information document and provide it to the approval authority.

2. *(►B)* If the new component's/STU's/system's properties lead to **higher** CO2/fuel-consumption values than the existing parent's, the **new** component/STU/system **becomes the new parent**.

### Article 19 — Subsequent changes relevant for the certification of CO2 emissions and fuel consumption related properties of components, separate technical units and systems
*(►B baseline, unamended in this range)*

1. The manufacturer notifies the approval authority of any **design or manufacturing-process changes** to certified components/STUs/systems that occur after certification (Article 17) and that **may have a non-negligible effect** on CO2/fuel-consumption-related properties.
2. On receiving that notification, the approval authority informs the manufacturer whether the affected components/STUs/systems **remain covered** by the existing certificate, or whether **additional testing** (Article 14) is needed to verify the changes' impact.
3. If not covered, the manufacturer must, **within one month** of that information, apply for a new certification or an extension (Article 18). **Failure to apply within that deadline, or rejection, results in withdrawal of the certificate.**

## CHAPTER 5 — CONFORMITY OF SIMULATION TOOL OPERATION, INPUT INFORMATION AND INPUT DATA (Articles 20–23)

### Article 20 — Responsibilities of the vehicle manufacturer, the approval authority and the Commission with regard to the conformity of simulation tool operation
*(title added by ►M1; content mixed ►B / ►M1 / ►M3)*

1. *(►B, amended ►M3)* The vehicle manufacturer must take necessary measures to ensure the licensed processes (Article 7) **continue to be adequate**.

   *(►M1, amended ►M3)* For **medium and heavy lorries** (**except He-HDV or PEV** [pure electric vehicles]), the vehicle manufacturer must perform the **verification testing procedure set out in Annex Xa** on a minimum number of vehicles per that Annex, point 3. The manufacturer must provide, **until 31 December of each year**, a test report to the approval authority for each vehicle tested (per Annex Xa, point 8), **keep the test reports for at least 10 years**, and make them available to the Commission and other Member States' approval authorities on request.

   *(Flagged cross-reference: the substantive content of the Annex Xa verification-testing procedure, including the "minimum number of vehicles," is outside this file's assigned line range (1–1992) and should be transcribed in the knowledge-base file covering Annex Xa.)*

2. *(►B, amended ►M3)* The approval authority performs, **four times per year**, an assessment (Annex II, point 2) to verify the manufacturer's processes remain adequate for all application cases/vehicle groups covered by the licence. This assessment also includes verification of input information/input data selection and **repetition of the manufacturer's simulations**.

   *(►M1)* Where a vehicle **fails** the Annex Xa verification testing procedure, the approval authority starts an **investigation** into the cause (per Annex Xa). On determining the cause, it informs the other Member States' approval authorities.
   - If the cause is linked to **operation of the simulation tool** → **Article 21** applies.
   - If the cause is linked to **certified CO2/fuel-consumption-related properties** of components/STUs/systems → **Article 23** applies.
   - If **no irregularities** are found in either, the approval authority reports the vehicle failure to the **Commission**, which investigates whether the **simulation tool itself or the verification testing procedure** caused the failure and whether an improvement is necessary.

### Article 21 — Remedial measures for the conformity of simulation tool operation
*(mixed ►B / ►M1 / ►M3)*

1. *(►B)* Where the approval authority finds (per Article 20(2)) that the manufacturer's processes are **not in accordance with the licence or this Regulation**, or **may lead to an incorrect determination** of CO2/fuel consumption, the approval authority requests a **plan of remedial measures**, to be submitted **no later than 30 calendar days** after receipt of that request (extendable by up to a further **30 calendar days** if the manufacturer demonstrates more time is needed).
2. *(►M3)* The plan applies to **all** application cases and vehicle groups identified by the approval authority in its request.
3. *(►M1, amended ►M3)* The approval authority **approves or rejects** the plan **within 30 calendar days** of receipt, and notifies the manufacturer and all other Member States of its decision.

   *(►M3)* The approval authority **may require** the vehicle manufacturer to issue a **new** manufacturer's records file, vehicle information file, customer information file **and** certificate of conformity, based on a new CO2/fuel-consumption determination reflecting the approved remedial measures.

   *(►M3)* The vehicle manufacturer must ensure the licensed processes continue to be adequate, and, for medium/heavy lorries, must perform the Annex Xa verification testing procedure on a minimum number of vehicles per that Annex, point 3.
4. *(►B)* The manufacturer is **responsible for the execution** of the approved plan.
5. *(►B)* Where the plan is **rejected**, or the approval authority finds remedial measures are **not being correctly applied**, it must take necessary measures to ensure conformity, **or withdraw the licence**.

### Article 22 — Responsibilities of the manufacturer and approval authority with regards to conformity of CO2 emissions and fuel consumption related properties of components, separate technical units and systems
*(mixed ►B / ►M3 / ►M4)*

1. *(►B, amended ►M3)* The manufacturer must take necessary measures (per **Annex IV to Regulation (EU) 2018/858**) to ensure the CO2/fuel-consumption-related properties of certified components/STUs/systems (Article 12(1)) **do not deviate** from the certified values. These measures also include the procedures laid down in:
   - Appendix 4 to Annex V (engines);
   - point 7 of Annex VI (transmissions);
   - points 5 and 6 of Annex VII (axles);
   - Appendix 6 to Annex VIII (air drag);
   - point 4 of Annex X (tyres);
   - *(►M3)* points 1 to 4 of Appendix 12 to Annex Xb (electric powertrain components);
   - *(►M4)* point 5 of Annex VIIa (wheel ends).

   Where properties of a family member were certified under Article 15(5), the **reference value for verification** is the one certified for **that family member** (not the family parent).

   Where a deviation is identified, the manufacturer must **immediately inform the approval authority**.

2. *(►B)* The manufacturer provides, **annually**, testing reports with the results of the above procedures to the approval authority that certified the family, and must make them available to the Commission on request.

3. *(►M3)* **Independent-oversight / cross-check rule:** the manufacturer must ensure that **at least 1 in every 25** of the above verification procedures — or, with an **exception for tyres**, **at least 1 procedure per year** — relating to a component/STU/system (or family) is **supervised by a different approval authority** than the one that originally certified it (per Article 16).

4. *(►B)* Any approval authority may **at any time** perform verifications at any manufacturer's/vehicle manufacturer's facilities, to check the properties do not deviate from certified values. The manufacturer/vehicle manufacturer must provide, **within 15 working days** of the request, all relevant documents, samples and materials needed.

### Article 23 — Remedial measures for the conformity of CO2 emissions and fuel consumption related properties of components, separate technical units and systems
*(mixed ►B / ►M1 / ►M3 / ►M4 — article number/heading placement per ►M4)*

1. *(►M1)* Where the approval authority finds (per Articles 20 and 22) that the manufacturer's conformity measures are **not adequate**, it requests a **plan of remedial measures**, submitted **no later than 30 calendar days** after the request (extendable up to a further **30 calendar days**, per ►B).
2. *(►M3)* The plan applies to **all** identified components/STUs/systems (or families).
3. *(►B)* The approval authority **approves or rejects** the plan **within 30 calendar days** of receipt and notifies the manufacturer and all other Member States.

   *(►M3)* The approval authority **may require** the vehicle manufacturer to issue a **new** manufacturer's records file, customer information file, vehicle information file **and** certificate of conformity, reflecting the approved remedial measures.
4. *(►B)* The manufacturer is responsible for execution of the approved plan.
5. *(►M3)* The manufacturer must **keep a record** of every component/STU/system **recalled and repaired or modified**, and of the workshop that performed the repair/modification. The approval authority has access to these records on request **during implementation and for 5 years after completion**. **The manufacturer must store these records for 10 years.**
6. *(►B)* Where the plan is rejected, or remedial measures are not being correctly applied, the approval authority takes necessary measures to ensure conformity, **or withdraws the certificate** on CO2/fuel-consumption-related properties.

## CHAPTER 6 — FINAL PROVISIONS (Articles 24–27)

### Article 24 — Application of the requirements
*(Article heading/content as substituted by ►M4; this replaced an earlier, differently-worded Article 24)*

> *(►M4)* "Without prejudice to Article 10(3) of this Regulation, where the obligations referred to in Article 9 of this Regulation have not been complied with, Member States shall consider certificates of conformity for type approved vehicles to be no longer valid for the purposes of Article 48 of Regulation (EU) 2018/858, and, for type approved vehicles and individually approved vehicles, shall prohibit the registration, sale or entry into service of vehicles in groups **1s, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 9, 10, 11, 12, 16, 31 to 40, 53 and 54**."
