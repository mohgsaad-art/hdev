# Dragør Kommune — Fleet Electrification Case Study

**Source file:** `Casehistorier_Fagfolk_templafy.pdf` (compendium; story: "Miljø og økonomi går hånd i hånd til gavn for klimaet i Dragør Kommune")
**Category:** Case study — fleet electrification (municipal elderly-care meal delivery)
**Original language:** Danish (translated to English below; proper nouns kept in Danish)
**One-line summary:** Dragør Kommune's care center Enggården replaced two aging diesel meal-delivery vans (which would have failed inspection) with 2 electric Nissan e-NV200 vans in May 2015, cutting CO2 by 6 tons/year and saving on fuel, green ownership tax, service and maintenance.

## Company Profile
- **Organization:** Dragør Kommune — specifically Omsorgscentret Enggården (Enggården Care Center), plus Vej og Gartner (Roads and Gardening) department
- **Sector:** Municipal elderly care (meal delivery / "udbringning af mad") and municipal roads/grounds maintenance
- **Fleet featured:** 2 Nissan e-NV200 (Enggården meal delivery vans), 1 Volkswagen e-up (Vej og Gartner department's driftsleder / operations manager vehicle)
- **Location:** Dragør Kommune
- **Person(s) quoted:** Eik Dahl Bidstrup — Borgmester (Mayor); Jørgen Krarup Jensen — Plan og Teknik (Planning and Technical department)
- **Website noted in source:** dragoer.dk

## Electrification Timeline
- **2010:** Dragør Kommune entered an agreement with Danmarks Naturfredningsforening (Danish Society for Nature Conservation) to become a "Klimakommune" (Climate Municipality), committing to reduce the municipality's CO2 emissions by 3% per year through to 2020.
- **December 2014:** The municipality's first EV, a Volkswagen e-up, purchased for use by the Vej og Gartner (Roads and Gardening) department's operations manager for supervising department tasks across the municipality.
- **May 2015:** Mayor Eik Dahl Bidstrup inaugurated two new electric Nissan e-NV200 vans for meal delivery from Omsorgscentret Enggården to elderly residents, replacing two old diesel vans that could no longer pass vehicle inspection (syn).
- Fleet analysis: Dragør Kommune has 30 vehicles in total, of which around half could favorably be replaced with EVs, in collaboration with Region Hovedstadens rejsehold (traveling task force).

## Financial Figures
| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| CO2 reduction from the 2 new delivery EVs | 6 tons CO2/year |
| Avoided vehicle-inspection repair cost (per old diesel van) | ca. 25,000 DKK/vehicle (cost the old vans would have needed to pass inspection) |
| Annual saving on fuel and green ownership tax (grøn ejerafgift) | 30,000–35,000 DKK/year |
| Annual saving on service and maintenance | Just under (små) 10,000 DKK/year |

## Operational Notes
- EVs are specifically well suited to Dragør's municipal driving profile: many short trips with frequent starts/stops, where (unlike combustion vehicles) short trips are not a problem for EVs.
- Benefits cited: improved local environment (no fossil fuel combustion, no harmful particulate emissions), improved work environment (automatic transmission is easier to operate, said to correlate with fewer workplace injuries in other municipalities' experience), significantly less cabin noise than conventional vehicles.
- Delivery vans include parking sensors and a rear-view camera/screen specifically to reduce reversing accidents and costly parking damage.
- EVs financed/acquired via Hovedstadsregionens elbilpartnerskab with Energistyrelsen support, part of a 500+ EV partnership across public/private partners.

(With the acquisition of the two EVs we've not only saved the significant money for getting the vans through inspection, we save 30,000-35,000 DKK a year on fuel and green ownership tax and just under 10,000 DKK on service and maintenance.)

They appreciate the driving comfort and the car's equipment and are pleased to drive without exhaust emissions. Healthy food, a green environment, and EVs go well together.)

## Future Plans
- Reduce municipal CO2 emissions by 3%/year through 2020 (per the 2010 Klimakommune agreement).
- Replace roughly half of the municipality's 30-vehicle fleet with EVs, per the fleet analysis conducted with Region Hovedstaden's rejsehold.
