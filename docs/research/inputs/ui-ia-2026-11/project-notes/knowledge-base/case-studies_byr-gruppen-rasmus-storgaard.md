# BYR Gruppen / Rasmus Storgaard (Private EV) — Case Study

**Source file:** `Casehistorier_Fagfolk_templafy.pdf` (compendium; story: "Elbilen har på mange måder overgået mine forventninger")
**Category:** Case study — individual/private EV adoption (company director, personal vehicle choice)
**Original language:** Danish (translated to English below; proper nouns kept in Danish)
**One-line summary:** BYR Gruppen director Rasmus Storgaard gave up his company petrol car in favor of privately buying a Tesla Model S, ordered in 2010 and delivered November 2013, and reports driving more (not less) since switching to electric.

## Company Profile
- **Organization/Individual:** Rasmus Storgaard — Director (direktør) of BYR Gruppen; this is a private/personal vehicle case, not a company fleet case
- **Vehicle:** Tesla Model S 85 Performance
- **Private driving need:** ca. 18,000 km/year
- **Location:** Family residence in Rødovre; family summer house 344 km away
- **Person(s) quoted:** Rasmus Storgaard; his wife Helene is referenced as an influence but not directly quoted

## Electrification Timeline
- Rasmus Storgaard replaced his petrol company car with a personally purchased electric Tesla Model S.
- Motivation: his wife Helene, described as very environmentally conscious, was determined the household's next car change would be electric.
- Storgaard researched the EV market and identified Tesla's new upcoming model as compelling.
- **2010:** Family ordered the Tesla Model S.
- **November 2013:** Family took delivery of their new electric car.

## Operational Notes
- Storgaard reports driving more since switching to the Tesla, finding it more enjoyable to drive.
- On long trips (e.g. the 344 km summer-house journey), the family takes a charging break, which doubles as a meal/rest stop for the children — described as more comfortable than driving straight through, particularly for the children.
- The cabin is noted as quiet enough for conversation without competing with engine noise.
- The family's three children (ages 2–5) are noted to enjoy sitting in the rear-facing seats and being able to see out and wave to other road users.

## Quotes
- Rasmus Storgaard: "Særligt min kone Helene er meget miljøbevidst og var indstillet på, at vi skulle have en elbil, næste gang vi skulle skifte bil." (My wife Helene in particular is very environmentally conscious and was determined that we'd get an EV the next time we changed cars.)
- Rasmus Storgaard: "Jeg undersøgte hvilke elbiler der var på markedet og fandt ud af, at Tesla var på vej med en ny spændende model. Det var med glæde at jeg kunne konstatere, at man faktisk kunne få en fed elbil." (I researched which EVs were on the market and found out Tesla was coming out with an exciting new model. I was happy to discover you could actually get a really cool EV.)
- Rasmus Storgaard: "Før kørte jeg 10 – 12.000 kilometer om året, men nu kører jeg mere. Det er lidt sjovere at køre nu." (Before I drove 10-12,000 km a year, but now I drive more. It's a bit more fun to drive now.)
- Rasmus Storgaard: "Elbilen har på mange måder overgået mine forventninger. Det bedste er, at den er så lydløs." (The EV has in many ways exceeded my expectations. The best part is how silent it is.) — this line is also used as the story's pulled headline quote.
- Rasmus Storgaard: "Den flytter nogle grænser for hvad du tænker om en bil og hvordan du oplever det, at køre i bil. En konventionel bil er pludselig gammeldags." (It shifts some boundaries for what you think about a car and how you experience driving. A conventional car suddenly feels old-fashioned.)
- Rasmus Storgaard, on the children's view of EVs: "Vores børn er bevidste om, at ikke alle biler er ens. Når de leger med biler er der én bil der er særlig sej, en som er den fede bil; det er en elbil." (Our children are aware that not all cars are the same. When they play with toy cars there's one that's especially cool, the awesome one; that's an EV.)
- Rasmus Storgaard, on the long trip: "Vi kan godt køre hele vejen, men vælger at tage en pause undervejs af hensyn til børnene. Så kan vi spise imens bilen får strøm... Faktisk er det mere behageligt end at køre i et stræk, særligt for børnene... Vi nyder stilheden og det at vi kan sidde og tale sammen under turen og ikke skal overdøve motorstøjen, som i gamle dage." (We could drive the whole way, but choose to take a break along the way for the children's sake. Then we can eat while the car charges... Actually it's more comfortable than driving straight through, especially for the children... We enjoy the quiet and being able to sit and talk during the trip without having to shout over engine noise like in the old days.)
