# Post Danmark (PostNord) — Fleet Electrification Case Study

**Source file:** `Casehistorier_Fagfolk_templafy.pdf` (compendium; story: "Post Danmark har mere end 100 års erfaring med kørsel i elbiler")
**Category:** Case study — fleet electrification (postal/parcel delivery, large scale)
**Original language:** Danish (translated to English below; proper nouns kept in Danish)
**One-line summary:** PostNord (owner of Post Danmark) states it has over 100 years of experience with electric vehicles, today running ca. 5,000 electric vehicles group-wide (mostly e-bikes/e-mopeds), 53 Mercedes-Benz Vito E-Cell vans in 14 Danish cities, with 100 more EVs incoming and a stated potential to convert up to 1,200 (70-100%) of its small vans to electric.

## Company Profile
- **Company:** Post Danmark A/S, part of the PostNord group (which also owns Swedish Posten AB)
- **Industry/sector:** Postal and parcel delivery
- **Fleet featured:** 53 Mercedes-Benz Vito E-Cell (current electric vans, deployed across 14 Danish cities); ca. 5,000 electric vehicles group-wide (PostNord), mostly e-bikes and e-mopeds ("elcykler og elknallerter"), with a growing share of full EVs
- **Annual driving need cited (Fakta box)** 30,000 km/year (context: per Post Danmark vehicle/segment, as printed)
- **Person(s) quoted:** Martin von Horsten — Distributionschef (Distribution Manager), Post Danmark; Jim Dudmish — Projektleder (Project Manager)

## Electrification Timeline
- Source headline claims "more than 100 years" of Post Danmark experience with electric vehicles — historical detail beyond this framing is not given in the source (i.e., no specific historical timeline of the early-20th-century EV usage is provided; this is presented as a headline claim only).
- In recent years, investment in electric vehicles has accelerated significantly ("Inden for de seneste år er det gået stærkt med investering i elkøretøjer").
- Currently (at time of writing): PostNord group-wide operates ca. 5,000 electric vehicles, the majority being e-bikes and e-mopeds, with a growing share of full electric vans/cars.
- Post Danmark specifically operates 53 Mercedes-Benz Vito E-Cell vans, deployed in 14 cities, selected for shorter routes matched to the vehicles' range.
- 100 additional EVs are being added as part of Post Danmark's participation in Region Hovedstadens elbilpartnerskab (a joint-procurement partnership for municipalities and companies).

## Financial Figures
- No specific DKK cost or savings figures are stated in this source.
- No CO2 tonnage figure is stated in this source; company-level target: reduce CO2 emissions by 40% between 2009 and 2020, as part of PostNord's broader vision to be "kundernes miljørigtige valg" (customers' environmentally sound choice).

## Operational Notes
- Employees receive thorough introduction/training before EVs are deployed operationally, including guidance on range, range-optimizing driving, use of the pre-heater (forvarmer), and automatic transmission.
- Vehicle selection matches route length to available EV range (shorter routes assigned to EVs).
- Reported strong staff enthusiasm: one EV loaned to a Swedish PostNord unit was frequently asked about regarding its return, according to Jim Dudmish.
- Cited user benefits: absence of engine noise is described as the single biggest gain for users, alongside gearless driving.

## Quotes
- Martin von Horsten (Distributionschef): "Investeringerne i elkøretøjer er en meget vigtig del af udviklingen af PostNords køretøjsflåde. Elkøretøjer er støjsvage, neutrale for nærmiljøet og en betydelig arbejdsmæssig forbedring. Det er endvidere vores foreløbige erfaring, at vores medarbejdere i meget høj grad motiveres af, at bidrage til, at etablere en grønnere distribution." (Investment in electric vehicles is a very important part of developing PostNord's vehicle fleet. Electric vehicles are quiet, neutral for the local environment, and a significant workplace improvement. It's also our preliminary experience that our employees are highly motivated to contribute to establishing greener distribution.)
- Jim Dudmish (Projektleder): "Vores vurdering er, at 2/3 af vores små varebiler kan skiftes til elbiler, det svarer til 1.200 elbiler." (Our assessment is that 2/3 of our small vans can be switched to EVs, corresponding to 1,200 EVs.)
- Jim Dudmish: "Medarbejderne er meget begejstrede og tilfredse med at køre i vores elbiler. Vi har netop haft lånt en til Sverige, og der blev der hele tiden spurgt til, hvornår den kom tilbage." (Employees are very enthusiastic and satisfied driving our EVs. We just had one loaned to Sweden, and people kept asking when it would come back.)
- Jim Dudmish: "Fraværet af støj er den største gevinst for brugerne og kørsel uden gearskifte er en anden væsentlig fordel." (The absence of noise is the biggest benefit for users, and gearless driving is another significant advantage.)

## Future Plans
- Add 100 more EVs via Hovedstadsregionens elbilpartnerskab.
- Continue toward the target of up to 70% (rising to 100% as technology/prices improve) of small vans converted to electric — a total potential of ca. 1,200 EVs (2/3 of the small-van fleet).
- Reduce group CO2 emissions by 40% between 2009 and 2020.
