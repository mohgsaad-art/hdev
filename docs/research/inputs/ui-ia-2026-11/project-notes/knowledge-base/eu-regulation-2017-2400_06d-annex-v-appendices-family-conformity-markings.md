## Appendix 3 — Engine CO2-Family ▼M3

### 1. Parameters defining the engine CO2-family

The engine CO2-family must comply with the membership criteria of point 5.2.3 of Annex 4 to UN R49. **An engine CO2-family may consist of only one engine.** Dual-fuel engines additionally comply with point 3.1.1 of Annex 15 to UN R49. Manufacturers may add criteria for more restrictive sub-families (not necessarily fuel-consumption-relevant). In addition, families must meet points 1.1–1.10 below.

**1.1 Combustion-relevant geometric data:** displacement/cylinder; number of cylinders; bore & stroke; combustion-chamber geometry & compression ratio; valve diameters & port geometry; fuel-injector design/position; cylinder-head design; piston & piston-ring design.

**1.2 Air-management components:** pressure-charging type (waste gate/VTG/2-stage/other) & thermodynamics; charge-air-cooling concept; valve-timing concept (fixed/partly flexible/flexible); EGR concept (cooled/uncooled, high/low pressure, control).

**1.3 Injection system.**

**1.4 Auxiliary/equipment propulsion concept** (mechanical/electrical/other).

**1.5 Waste heat recovery system(s) ▼M3:** type; test setup (point 3.1.6); turbine/evaporator/expander/condenser/pump type. For **all other engines** in the family vs. the CO2-parent engine:
- 1.5.8 `LEW` ≥ CO2-parent's `LEW`
- 1.5.9 `LET` ≥ CO2-parent's `LET`
- 1.5.10 `LHE` ≥ CO2-parent's `LHE`
- 1.5.11 `LEC` ≤ CO2-parent's `LEC`
- 1.5.12 `LCE` ≤ CO2-parent's `LCE`
- 1.5.13 `pcond` ≥ CO2-parent's `pcond`
- 1.5.14 `Pcool` ≥ CO2-parent's `Pcool`

**1.6 Aftertreatment system:** reagent-dosing characteristics; catalyst/DPF arrangement, material, coating; HC-dosing characteristics.

**1.7 Full load curve:**
- 1.7.1 CO2-parent engine's full-load-curve torque at each speed ≥ **all other** family engines' torque at the same speed, over the whole recorded speed range.
- 1.7.2 The **lowest-power-rated** engine's full-load torque at each speed ≤ **all other** family engines' torque at the same speed, over the whole recorded speed range.
- 1.7.3 **▼M3:** torque values within a **tolerance band** of **+40 Nm or +4% of the CO2-parent engine's torque** at that speed (whichever greater) are considered equal.

**1.8 Characteristic engine test speeds:**
- **▼M1** 1.8.1 CO2-parent's declared `nidle` ≤ all other family engines' `nidle`.
- 1.8.2 Other engines' `n95h` must not deviate from the CO2-parent's `n95h` by more than **± 3%**.
- 1.8.3 Other engines' `n57` must not deviate from the CO2-parent's `n57` by more than **± 3%**.

**1.9 Minimum number of points in the fuel consumption map:** 1.9.1 — all family engines must have **≥ 54 mapping points** located below their respective full-load curve (4.3.1).

**1.10 Variation in GERWHTC ▼M3:** 1.10.1 — for dual-fuel engines, the difference between the highest and lowest GERWHTC within the family must **not exceed 10%**.

**1.11 ▼M4 Special provisions for diesel engines tested with B100:** 1.11.1 — all family engines must be capable of running on **pure B100** and on the **exact same range** of biodiesel blends declared at point 3.2.2.2.1 of the Information Document.

### 2. Choice of the CO2-parent engine

2.1 — **Highest power rating** of all engines within the engine CO2-family.

## Appendix 4 — Conformity of CO2 Emissions and Fuel Consumption Related Properties (Conformity of Production) ▼M3

### 1. General provisions

1.1 Conformity checked against the Appendix-1 certificate and Appendix-2 information-document description.
1.2 Certificates with extensions: tests use the engines described in the relevant extension's information package.
1.3 Engines tested must be taken from series production meeting point 3's selection criteria.
1.4 Tests may use applicable **market fuels**; at the manufacturer's request, the **reference fuels** of point 3.2 (Part A) may be used instead.
1.5 For gas engines (NG/LPG) tested with market fuel: manufacturer must demonstrate appropriate determination of gas-fuel composition for NCV (point 4) by good engineering judgement.

### 2. Number of engines and CO2-families to be tested

2.1 **nCOP,base** = 0.05% of all engines produced in the past production year within scope, rounded to nearest whole number.
2.2 Minimum **nCOP,base = 30**, notwithstanding 2.1.
2.3 **nCOP,fam** (number of CO2-families tested annually) = nCOP,base ÷ 10, rounded to nearest whole number.
2.4 If a manufacturer has fewer CO2-families than nCOP,fam, **nCOP,fam** = that manufacturer's total number of families.

### 3. Selection of engine CO2-families to be tested

The **first two** families selected = those with the **highest production volumes**. Remaining families = **randomly selected**, agreed between manufacturer and approval authority.

### 4. Test run to be performed

**▼M1:** **nCOP,min** (minimum engines tested per family) = nCOP,base ÷ nCOP,fam, rounded to nearest integer; **bounded: if < 4 → set to 4; if > 19 → set to 19.**

A minimum of `nCOP,min` engines per selected family must be tested to reach a pass decision (point 9). Assignment of test runs to specific engines within a family is **random**, agreed between manufacturer and approval authority.

**▼M4:** test engines equipped with OBFCM must carry that device, as in point 3.1.2 (Part A).

Conformity is verified by testing engines in the **WHSC test** (point 4.3.4, Part B). All Annex boundary conditions apply, **except:**
1. Laboratory test conditions (point 3.1.1, Part A) are **recommended, not mandatory**; deviations should be minimised by good engineering judgement.
2. If **B7 (Diesel/CI)** reference fuel used: NCV determination (point 3.2) **not required**.
3. If market fuel or non-B7 reference fuel used: NCV determined per Table 1 standards (Part A), but with **only one** independent lab (not two) — except gas engines. NCV for reference gas fuels (G25/GR, LPG Fuel B) **▼M1** calculated per Table 1 standards from the fuel analysis submitted by the reference-fuel supplier. For market/reference **hydrogen**, NCV calculated per Table 1 from the fuel supplier's analysis.
4. Lubricating oil = the one filled during production; **not changed** for conformity testing.

### 5. Run-in of newly manufactured engines

5.1 Tests on newly manufactured engines with **max 15 hours** run-in before testing starts.
5.2 At manufacturer's request: engines run-in up to **max 125 hours**; running-in conducted by the manufacturer with **no adjustments**.
5.3 If running-in per 5.2 requested, applied to either:
   (a) all tested engines, or
   **▼M3** (b) a newly produced engine, with an **evolution coefficient** determined as:
   - A. SFC measured over WHSC once at ≤15 h run-in (5.1) and again at ≤125 h (5.2), on the first tested engine.
   - B. SFCWHSC determined per point 5.3.3 (Part C) for both tests.
   - C. Both values corrected per points 7.2–7.4 of this Appendix for the fuel used in each test.
   - D. **Evolution coefficient = (corrected SFC of 2nd test) / (corrected SFC of 1st test)** — may be < 1.
   - E. **▼M4** For dual-fuel engines (NG/LPG): point D does **not** apply; instead, evolution coefficient = (specific CO2 emissions, 2nd test) / (specific CO2 emissions, 1st test), per point 6.1 — may be < 1.
   - F. **▼M4** For dual-fuel engines (hydrogen): point D does not apply; evolution coefficient = (specific energy consumption, 2nd test) / (specific energy consumption, 1st test), per point 6.2 — may be < 1.
5.4 If 5.3(b) applied: subsequent engines selected for conformity testing are **not** run-in; their 15-hour SFCWHSC (or specific CO2/energy consumption for dual-fuel) is **multiplied by the evolution coefficient**.
5.5 **▼M3/▼M4** Values to use in the 5.4 scenario: (a) for the engine used to determine the evolution coefficient — the **2nd-test** value; (b) for other engines — the 15-hour value **× evolution coefficient**.
5.6 **▼M3** Instead of 5.2–5.5, a **generic evolution coefficient of 0.99** may be used at the manufacturer's request, applied by multiplying the 15-hour SFCWHSC (or CO2/energy consumption) value. **▼M4**
5.7 If the evolution coefficient (5.3(b)) is determined using an engine-family parent per points 5.2.3/5.2.4 of Annex 4 to UN R49, it may be **carried across** to all CO2-family members belonging to the same UN-R49 engine family (point 5.2.3 of Annex 4).

### 6. Target value for assessment of conformity ▼M4

Target value = corrected SFCWHSC (SFCWHSC,corr) [g/kWh], per point 5.3.3 (Part C), as documented in the Appendix-2 information document for the specific engine tested.

**6.1 Dual-fuel engines (NG/LPG) ▼M3/▼M4:** target value = sum of the two fuels' SFCWHSC,corr values, **each multiplied by its CO2 emission factor** (Table 1 below).

**Table 1 — CO2 emission factors of fuel types** ▼M3/▼M4

| Fuel type / engine type | Reference fuel type | CO2 emission factor [g CO2 / g fuel] |
|---|---|---|
| Diesel / CI | B7 | 3.13 |
| LPG / PI | LPG Fuel B | 3.02 |
| Natural Gas / PI or Natural Gas / CI | G25 or GR | 2.73 |
| Diesel / CI ▼M4 | B100 | 2.83 |

**6.2 Dual-fuel engines (hydrogen) ▼M4:** target value = sum of the two fuels' SFCWHSC,corr values, each **multiplied by its NCVstd (point 5.3.3.1, Part C)** and then **by 0.001** — giving specific energy consumption over WHSC.

### 7. Actual value for assessment of conformity

7.1 SFCWHSC determined per point 5.3.3 (Part C) from point-4 test runs; manufacturer may request modification per points 5.3–5.6.
7.2 If market fuel used (point 1.4): adjusted to SFCWHSC,corr per point 5.3.3.1 (Part C).
7.3 **▼M3** If reference fuel used: special provisions of point 5.3.3.2 (Part C) applied.
7.3.a Dual-fuel engines: point 5.3.3.3 (Part C) applied in addition to 7.2/7.3.
7.4 Measured gaseous-pollutant emissions over the WHSC adjusted by applicable **deterioration factors (DFs)** per the EC type-approval certificate (Reg. (EU) No 582/2011).
7.5 **▼M3** Actual value = corrected SFCWHSC,corr per 7.2/7.3.
7.6 **▼M4** Dual-fuel (NG/LPG): point 7.5 does not apply; actual value = sum of the two fuels' specific CO2 emissions over WHSC per point 6.1, using the SFCWHSC,corr values from 7.4.
7.7 **▼M4** Dual-fuel (hydrogen): point 7.5 does not apply; actual value = sum of the two fuels' specific energy consumption over WHSC per point 6.2, using SFCWHSC,corr from 7.4.

### 8. Limit for conformity of one single test

- **Diesel (B7 or B100):** limit = target value (point 6) **+ 4%**.
- **Single fuel other than diesel, and dual-fuel engines:** limit = target value (point 6) **+ 5%**.
- **8.1 ▼M4** Dual-fuel hydrogen engines: additional limit on specific diesel consumption over WHSC (SFCWHSC,corr) = target value (point 6) **+ 4 g/kWh tolerance**.

### 9. Assessment of conformity

**▼M3** 9.1 — WHSC emission test results (point 7.4) must meet (else the test is **void** for conformity purposes): (a) limits in Annex I to Regulation (EC) No 595/2009; (b) dual-fuel engines: limits in point 5 of Annex XVIII to Regulation (EU) No 582/2011. (All gaseous pollutants **except ammonia**.)

9.2 — A single engine test is **non-conforming** if the actual value (point 7) exceeds the point-8 limit. **▼M4** Additionally, for dual-fuel hydrogen engines: also non-conforming if the actual specific diesel consumption (SFCWHSC,corr, point 7) exceeds the point-8.1 limit.

9.3 — Statistical test procedure (sequential sampling): at the nth test, the cumulative number of non-conforming tests (9.2) is compared to Table 4 (pass/fail decision numbers for the sample size) of Appendix 3 to UN R49:
- (a) cumulative non-conforming ≤ pass-decision number → **pass**.
- **▼M4** (b) cumulative non-conforming ≥ fail-decision number → **fail**.
- (c) otherwise → test one more engine, repeat.

9.4 — If neither pass nor fail is reached, the manufacturer may stop testing at any time; a **fail** decision is then recorded.

## Appendix 5 — Determination of Power Consumption of Engine Components ▼B

### 1. Fan

Engine torque measured at engine **motoring**, with and without fan engaged:

(i) Fan installed per product instructions before test start.
(ii) **Warm-up:** per manufacturer's recommendation / good engineering judgement (e.g. 20 min at mode 9, Table 1 of point 7.2.2 of Annex 4 to UN R49).
**▼M1** (iii) **Stabilisation (fan disengaged):** minimum operator demand (motoring) at speed `npref` for **130 ± 2 s**, fan disengaged (`nfan_disengage < 0.75 × nengine × rfan`). First **60 ± 1 s** = stabilisation (actual speed held within ± 5 min⁻¹ of `npref`).
(iv) **Measurement (fan disengaged):** following **60 ± 1 s**: actual speed within ± 2 min⁻¹ of `npref`, coolant temperature within ± 5°C; torque for motoring with fan disengaged, fan speed, and engine speed recorded as the **average over this 60 ± 1 s** period. Remaining 10 ± 1 s for data post-processing.
(v) **Optional repeat warm-up:** at manufacturer's request / good engineering judgement (e.g. if temperature dropped >5°C).
(vi) **Stabilisation (fan engaged):** as (iii) but fan engaged (`nfan_engage > 0.9 × nengine × rfan`), 130 ± 2 s, first 60 ± 1 s stabilisation within ± 5 min⁻¹ of `npref`.
(vii) **Measurement (fan engaged):** as (iv), fan engaged, averaged over 60 ± 1 s.
(viii) Steps (iii)–(vii) **repeated at speeds n95h and nhi** (instead of `npref`), with optional warm-up (v) before each if needed to maintain coolant temperature stability (± 5°C).
(ix) If the **standard deviation** of all calculated `Ci` (equation below) across the three speeds `npref`, `n95h`, `nhi` is **≥ 3%**, measurement must be performed at **all** engine speeds defining the FCMC mapping grid (point 4.3.5.2.1, Part B).

**Fan constant (per speed):**

> **Ci = (MDfan_disengage − MDfan_engage) / [(nfan_engage² − nfan_disengage²) × 10⁻⁶]**

where `Ci` = fan constant at a given engine speed; `MDfan_disengage` / `MDfan_engage` = measured motoring torque with fan disengaged/engaged [Nm]; `nfan_engage` / `nfan_disengage` = fan speed with fan engaged/disengaged [min⁻¹]; **▼M1** `rfan` = ratio of the engine-side fan-clutch speed to crankshaft speed.

- If std. dev. of `Ci` at the three speeds `npref`, `n95h`, `nhi` is **< 3%**: use the **average** `Cavg-fan` over the three speeds.
- If std. dev. is **≥ 3%**: use **individual values** `Cind-fan,i` per engine speed (point ix); interpolate **linearly** between them for the actual engine speed to get `Cfan`.

**Engine torque for driving the fan:**

> **Mfan = Cfan × nfan² × 10⁻⁶**

where `Mfan` = engine torque for driving the fan [Nm]; `Cfan` = `Cavg-fan` or the interpolated `Cind-fan,i` corresponding to `nengine`.

Mechanical power consumed by the fan calculated from `Mfan` and actual engine speed; power and torque accounted for per point 3.1.2 (Part A).

### 2. Electric components/equipment

Electric power supplied externally to electric engine components is **measured**, then corrected to mechanical power by dividing by a **generic efficiency of 0.65**. This mechanical power and corresponding torque accounted for per point 3.1.2 (Part A).
