# Buus Anlægsgartner — Fleet Electrification Case Study

**Source file:** `CASE_Buus-FINAL.pdf` (also published as `CASE_Buus-FINAL 1.pdf` — see Duplicate Note below)
**Category:** Case study — fleet electrification
**Original language:** Danish (translated to English below; proper nouns kept in Danish)
**One-line summary:** A landscaping/grounds-maintenance company (anlægsgartner) that began electrifying its van fleet in 2015 with Nissan e-NV200s has seen employee skepticism turn into enthusiastic preference for EVs as company cars.

## Company Profile
- **Company:** Buus Anlægsgartner (landscaping/grounds-maintenance company; "anlægsgartner" = landscape gardening contractor)
- **Industry/sector:** Landscaping / grounds maintenance ("Vi arbejder med naturen" — we work with nature)
- **Fleet size:** 30 vans and company cars on electric ("30 VAREBILER OG FIRMABILER PÅ EL")
- **Employees:** 150 employees at peak season ("i højsæsonen")
- **Locations:** Domicile/offices in Aarhus and Aalborg; charging yard at Brabrand
- **Person(s) quoted:** Michael Falkenberg — Sales consultant & department head (salgskonsulent & afdelingsleder)
- **Publication year of case:** 2021

## Electrification Timeline
- **2015:** Buus purchased its first 4 electric vans — Nissan e-NV200 — despite known limitations in capacity and range at the time. Described as "not many electric vans to choose from" at that point.
- **2019:** New Citroën e-Jumpy vans ordered as diesel vans continued to be phased out in favor of stronger new EV models entering the market.
- **In progress (as of 2021):** A Citroën electric flatbed/load-bed van with trailer capability was being built in France — can tow up to 1,000 kg; Buus was waiting for this model so it could also replace its large diesel vehicles, including a 3-tonne excavator ("gravkoen").
- **Company car policy shift:** A year before the case was written (i.e. ~2020), most employees choosing a company car opted for hybrid. Within the first three months of 2021, this flipped completely — electric became the top choice for employees.

## Operational Notes
- Leadership approached EV adoption analytically rather than reactively: assessed actual driving needs, available EV models, and charging feasibility before committing ("Hvad er kørselsbehovet, hvilke elbiler kan vi få, og hvordan kan vi lade").
- EV adoption for company cars was framed as an offer/inspiration, not a mandate — employees chose freely between hybrid and electric options, and adoption naturally shifted toward EV once comfort/experience with the vehicles grew.
- Charging is depot-based: fleet returns to base at shift change (16:00) and connects to the 8 on-site chargers.
- No V2X or grid-management details mentioned. No specific charger power (kW) rating is stated in the source.
- Business driver noted: growing demand from municipalities ("kommuner") and companies for suppliers who act environmentally responsibly.

## Quotes
- Michael Falkenberg: "Hvis jeg for et år siden havde fortalt medarbejderne, at vores dieselbiler skulle erstattes af elbiler, var jeg blevet lagt ned." (If I had told employees a year ago that our diesel vehicles would be replaced with electric ones, I would have been shot down.)
- Michael Falkenberg: "I ledergruppen var vi enige om, at elbiler SKAL fungere. Vi arbejder med naturen og ønsker at være miljøbevidste." (In the leadership team we agreed that EVs MUST work. We work with nature and want to be environmentally conscious.)
- Michael Falkenberg: "Dengang i 2015 var der ikke mange elvarebiler at vælge imellem. Vi valgte Nissan e-NV200 vel vidende, at kapacitet og distance var en begrænsning. Men vi tog chancen og købte fire biler, og det har vi aldrig fortrudt." (Back in 2015 there weren't many electric vans to choose from. We chose the Nissan e-NV200 knowing full well that capacity and range were a limitation. But we took the chance and bought four vans, and we've never regretted it.)
- Michael Falkenberg: "For et år siden valgte de fleste hybrid-løsningen, men i løbet af de første tre måneder i 2021 vendte billedet fuldkomment. I dag har elbilen første prioritet hos vores medarbejdere, og det havde jeg ikke set komme så hurtigt." (A year ago most chose the hybrid option, but during the first three months of 2021 the picture flipped completely. Today the electric car is the first priority for our employees, and I hadn't seen that coming so fast.)
- Michael Falkenberg: "Jeg kan ikke finde et eneste argument for at fortsætte med dieselbiler. Vi har alternativerne allerede i dag, og der er kun fordele ved at bruge dem." (I can't find a single argument for continuing with diesel vehicles. We already have the alternatives today, and there are only advantages to using them.)

## Future Plans
- Continued replacement of remaining diesel vans as stronger EV models become available, specifically targeting large diesel vehicles (e.g. those towing a 3-tonne excavator) once the Citroën electric flatbed-with-trailer model becomes available.
- No explicit numeric target or year for 100% fleet electrification stated in source.
