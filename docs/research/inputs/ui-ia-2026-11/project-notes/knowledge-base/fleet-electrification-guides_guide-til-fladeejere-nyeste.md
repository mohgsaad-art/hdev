# Guide for Fleet Owners (2019 Edition): "Acquisition of Electric Vehicles & Charging Stations"

**Source file:** `Guide-Til-flaadeejere-nyeste.pdf`
**Category:** Fleet electrification guide / policy report
**Original language:** Danish (translated to English below; proper nouns kept in Danish)
**One-line summary:** The original 2019 edition of the "Guide til Flådeejere" ("Anskaffelse af elbiler & ladestandere"), produced by consultancy **Fremsyn** on behalf of and in collaboration with Region Hovedstaden's Copenhagen Electric, covering EV benefits, charging basics, a passenger-car/van market table, charging-station economics, and a 4-stage advisory process.

## COMPARISON WITH `Guide til Flådeejere.pdf` (companion file)

**Verdict: These are two DIFFERENT editions of the same guide, NOT duplicates.** Despite this file's filename containing "nyeste" (Danish for "newest"), its cover page explicitly states **"2019"**, and its final page carries an explicit colophon: *"Guiden er udarbejdet af Fremsyn på vegne af og i samarbejde med Region Hovedstadens elbilindsats, Copenhagen Electric. Udarbejdelsen er baseret på Fremsyns erfaringer med rådgivning af grøn omstilling i transport- og energisektoren."* (The guide was prepared by Fremsyn on behalf of and in collaboration with Region Hovedstaden's EV initiative, Copenhagen Electric, based on Fremsyn's experience advising on green transition in the transport and energy sector.) It also directs readers to order a fleet analysis at **www.fremsyn.net** and to an updated vehicle overview at **www.fremsyn.net/flaadeanalyser**.

The companion file `Guide til Flådeejere.pdf` (processed separately in this knowledge base) has no visible publisher colophon or explicit year in the extracted text, but contains a reference to a "July 2020" EV-registration statistic in the (identical) CSR/climate section, and contains a **materially different, apparently updated dataset**. Note: the filename "nyeste" most likely reflects that this was the file hosted at the persistent URL `elbilviden.dk/.../Guide-Til-flaadeejere-nyeste.pdf` (confirmed by a footnote URL in the `Virksomhedsguide 2023.pdf` source, uploaded there in September 2020 per the URL's `/uploads/2020/09/` path) — i.e., "nyeste" was the filename slug for "whichever is the current version," not a guarantee that the PDF content itself was the newest. Content evidence indicates this 2019-dated file is actually the **earlier** edition, and `Guide til Flådeejere.pdf` is a **later revision**.

### Conclusion on duplication

The two files share the same overall structure, section order, professional-advisory 4-stage framework, budget example structure (same DKK 40,000/20,000/31,500/20,000-60,000 line items), and the same three testimonial quotes (AFA JCDecaux, KLS PurePrint, De Grønne Elektrikere) verbatim. However, they are **not identical documents** — the newer-looking file (`Guide til Flådeejere.pdf`) updates/replaces the vehicle market table (adds a full truck segment, changes AC/DC charging-power ranges, updates several car/van specs) and slightly restructures the charging-cost example tables and budget table subscription/refund columns. Treat `Guide til Flådeejere.pdf` as the more current edition for vehicle-spec purposes, and this file (2019, Fremsyn) as useful primarily for its explicit authorship/date attribution and its distinct DC/AC charging-power figures, which may reflect what "hurtigladning"/"lynladning" meant contemporaneously in 2019 Denmark.

The structural sections are the same as the companion guide: introduction, EV benefits, test-driving recommendation, needs assessment (daily km, annual km, annual fuel use), economic reallocation, climate/environment, CSR, charging types (AC/DC), charging-time examples (BMW i3, Nissan e-NV200), vehicle market tables, charging-station installation economics, lease vs. own charging solutions, subscription/refund/smart-charging/access-control/public-charging concepts, TCO worked example (Nissan e-NV200 vs. Nissan NV200), emissions comparison, testimonials, and 4-stage professional advisory process. See the companion document for the full content of these shared sections (this file's version of that content is substantively the same prose, only the tables noted above differ).

### This edition's distinctive additions
- **Explicit publisher/date colophon** (see above) — the companion file lacks this.
- **www.fremsyn.net/flaadeanalyser** cited as the place to find an "updated overview" of EVs on the market — implying this PDF's own vehicle table was already understood to age quickly and be superseded by the website.
- Slightly different phrasing in a few places, e.g., "fossilbiler" (fossil-fuel vehicles) is used generically in places where the companion file says "benzin- og dieselbiler."
- The EV-benefits bullet list includes an extra bullet not in the companion file: **"Vær med på den grønne bølge"** (Be part of the green wave).
- The "Er der strøm nok?" (Is there enough power?) section omits the specific mention of Ampere-based billing methods described in the companion file's equivalent section (shorter treatment here).
