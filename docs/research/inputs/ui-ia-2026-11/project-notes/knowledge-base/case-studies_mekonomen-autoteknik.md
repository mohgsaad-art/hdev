# Mekonomen Autoteknik (ES Motor) — EV Demonstration Case Study

**Source file:** `Casehistorier_Fagfolk_templafy.pdf` (compendium; story: "Mekonomen Autoteknik møder elbilen")
**Category:** Case study — EV demonstration/awareness event (automotive workshop chain)
**Original language:** Danish (translated to English below; proper nouns kept in Danish)
**One-line summary:** A Brøndby car workshop under the Mekonomen Autoteknik chain hosted the "Mød elbilen" demonstration and extended its trial by 14 extra days after 200+ visitors (staff, customers, politicians) test-drove the EVs, subsequently planning to acquire 15 EVs as workshop loaner vehicles.

## Company Profile
- **Company:** Mekonomen Autoteknik – ES Motor, Vallensbækvej, Brøndby
- **Industry/sector:** Automotive repair/workshop; ES Motor has operated as an authorized dealer/workshop for 6 different car brands over more than 70 years
- **Chain scale:** Mekonomen Autoteknik is described as Scandinavia's largest workshop chain, with 1,100+ affiliated workshops, of which 200+ are in Denmark
- **Person(s) quoted:** Mads Grotenberg — co-owner of ES Motor, presenting himself as proud owner of Mekonomen Autoteknik – ES Motor

## Electrification Timeline
- Awareness of the "Mød elbilen" demonstration project came about by chance ("ved en tilfældighed"). Since encountering the modern EVs in the Region Hovedstaden demonstration package, interest in electric vehicles at the workshop has grown substantially.
- Initial expectation before the trial was low ("vi havde en forventning til, at det var en Ellert i en pæn indpakning" — roughly, "we expected it to be a glorified golf-cart in nice packaging"), but this was revised significantly upward after direct experience.
- Demo EVs were tested not only by staff but by customers, suppliers, business people, and several politicians and parliamentary candidates ("folketingskandidater") at various events held on-site in Brøndby.
- The workshop was pleased enough with the demo vehicles to extend the trial period by an additional 14 days beyond the standard term, during which time Mads Grotenberg estimates more than 200 people got behind the wheel of the demo EVs.

## Operational Notes
- The workshop already services a number of EVs and plug-in hybrids, including many EVs from Københavns Kommune (City of Copenhagen).
- Business driver: the company states it must keep pace with technological development and train its mechanics to service new vehicle types, including EVs ("Vi skal naturligvis følge med udviklingen og uddanne vores mekanikere til at kunne servicere de nye biler, herunder også elbiler").
- The planned 15 EVs will specifically serve as loaner cars given to customers while their own vehicle is being serviced — both an operational and a marketing/awareness move.

## Quotes
- Mads Grotenberg: "Vi skal naturligvis følge med udviklingen og uddanne vores mekanikere til at kunne servicere de nye biler, herunder også elbiler." (We must naturally keep pace with developments and train our mechanics to be able to service the new cars, including EVs.)
- Mads Grotenberg: "Mødet med elbilerne blev lidt af en øjenåbner, vi havde en forventning til, at det var en Ellert i en pæn indpakning, men vi blev klogere." (The meeting with the EVs was a bit of an eye-opener; we expected it to be a dressed-up novelty car, but we learned better.)
- Mads Grotenberg: "Vi blev så glade for elbilerne, at vi forlængede vores testperiode med yderligere 14 dage og jeg tror, at vi i perioden har haft mere end 200 mennesker bag rattet i elbilerne." (We became so fond of the EVs that we extended our test period by another 14 days, and I believe that during that period we had more than 200 people behind the wheel of the EVs.)

## Future Plans
- Acquisition of 15 new EVs for use as customer loaner/courtesy vehicles at the workshop.
- Continued belief that EVs "are here to stay" and represent the direction the automotive repair industry must move in.
