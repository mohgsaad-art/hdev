# Ballerup Hjemmepleje — Fleet Electrification Case Study

**Source file:** `Casehistorier_Fagfolk_templafy.pdf` (compendium; story: "Ballerup Hjemmepleje går forrest i at skabe en grønnere hverdag")
**Category:** Case study — fleet electrification (municipal home care)
**Original language:** Danish (translated to English below; proper nouns kept in Danish)
**One-line summary:** Every third vehicle in Ballerup Kommune's home care fleet is electric, following delivery of 12 new Renault Zoe EVs on 25 June 2014 under the Capital Region's EV partnership program.

## Company Profile
- **Organization:** Hjemmeplejen i Ballerup Kommune (Home Care, Ballerup Municipality)
- **Sector:** Municipal home care
- **Total fleet:** 34 vehicles, of which 14 are hybrids and (after this delivery) 12 are electric — electric vehicles make up 35% of the fleet ("Elbilerne udgør 35 pct. af bilflåden")
- **Additional fleet:** 28 e-bikes (elcykler) used for short trips to residents daily
- **Location:** Ballerup Kommune
- **Person(s) quoted:** Jesper Würtzen (A) — Mayor (borgmester); Peter Sørensen — Operations Manager (driftsleder); Celina Dechau — Assistant and daily user of the EVs

## Electrification Timeline
- **25 June 2014:** 12 new Renault Zoe EVs delivered to Ballerup Kommune's home care fleet ("Solen skinnede fra en skyfri himmel den 25. juni 2014").
- Ballerup Kommune is certified as a "Grøn Transport-kommune" (Green Transport Municipality) and has committed to following the Danish Road Traffic Agency's (Trafikstyrelsen) recommendations for green transport and to reducing CO2 emissions from work-related driving.
- Prior to the EV delivery, home care already operated a number of hybrid vehicles.
- Vehicles were purchased via "Hovedstadsregionens elbilpartnerskab" (Capital Region's EV Partnership) with support from Energistyrelsen (Danish Energy Agency); this partnership covers more than 500 EVs across public and private partners.

## Operational Notes
- Employees received a short introductory course covering tips/tricks and points of attention for driving electric vs. a conventional petrol car.
- EVs quickly became the preferred vehicle type among staff, over conventional petrol cars.
- 28 e-bikes are used daily for short trips to residents, alongside the car fleet.

## Quotes
- Mayor Jesper Würtzen (A): "Elbiler passer godt ind i vores fokus på grøn transport." (EVs fit well with our focus on green transport.)
- Mayor Jesper Würtzen (A), after a test drive in one of the new EVs: "Hjemmeplejen i Ballerup Kommune er med deres nye elbiler med til at gå forrest og vise vejen til en grønnere hverdag, som forhåbentlig kan være til inspiration for mange andre." (Home care in Ballerup Kommune, with their new EVs, is helping lead the way to a greener everyday life, which hopefully can inspire many others.)
- Operations Manager Peter Sørensen: "Vi opnår en reduceret miljøbelastning for vores kørsel ud til borgerne og økonomisk tegner det til langt lavere driftsudgifter. The EVs have met all our requirements and expectations.)
- Celina Dechau (Assistant, daily user): "elbilerne kører fantastisk. De er kvikkere end benzinbilerne, lydløse og let kørte takket være automat gear." (The EVs drive fantastically. They're quicker than the petrol cars, silent, and easy to drive thanks to the automatic gearbox.)
- Unattributed staff sentiment quoted directly: "Personalet vil helst køre i elbilerne!" (The staff prefer to drive the EVs!)

## Future Plans
- The electric share of the fleet is expected to increase over the coming years based on initial positive experience ("en andel, som efter de første erfaringer at dømme, vil stige hen over de kommende år").
