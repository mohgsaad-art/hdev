# Frederiksberg Kommune (Elskraldebil) — Fleet Electrification Case Study

**Source file:** `Casehistorier_Fagfolk_templafy.pdf` (compendium; story: "Gode erfaringer med elskraldebil på Frederiksberg")
**Category:** Case study — heavy-duty electric vehicle (electric waste collection truck)
**Original language:** Danish (translated to English below; proper nouns kept in Danish)
**One-line summary:** Frederiksberg Kommune's electric garbage truck trial was successful enough that a second unit was ordered for service by end of 2016, despite 3 tonnes less payload capacity than diesel trucks, and after reducing battery size from 255 kWh to 180 kWh to recover 1 tonne of payload.

## Company Profile
- **Organization:** Frederiksberg Kommune — municipal waste collection
- **Sector:** Municipal waste management
- **Fleet featured:** 1 electric garbage truck (elskraldebil), used alongside the municipality's conventional diesel garbage trucks; a second unit ordered for deployment by end of 2016
- **Location:** Frederiksberg Kommune

## Electrification Timeline
- The electric garbage truck was trialled in daily waste collection operations on equal footing with the municipality's conventional diesel garbage trucks.
- During the trial, the municipality found the originally specified 255 kWh battery was more than needed and could be reduced to 180 kWh — a change that recovered roughly 1 tonne of additional payload capacity and reduced the number of trips to the incineration plant (forbrændingen).
- Frederiksberg Kommune's final trial report ("slutrapport") states the electric garbage truck performed flawlessly in daily operations, to strong staff satisfaction.
- A trial of earlier-than-normal departure — from 05:30 instead of the normal 06:00 — was run for the electric truck and deemed a success specifically because of its quiet operation; the municipality is now seeking permanent approval for this earlier departure time.
- **Result:** based on the positive trial, a second electric garbage truck was ordered, to enter service toward the end of 2016.

## Operational Notes
- The electric truck is popular among staff who normally drive diesel garbage trucks and are exposed to diesel engine noise all day — they specifically request/prefer the electric unit.
- Reduced battery size (180 kWh instead of 255 kWh) directly improved operational efficiency by restoring payload capacity and reducing trips to the incineration facility.
- Early/quiet departure (05:30) was made possible specifically because of the truck's low noise levels, and the municipality is pursuing permanent regulatory approval for this earlier start time.
- Benefits cited in the municipality's conclusion: better work environment (arbejdsmiljø) and reduced environmental impact (mindre miljøbelastning), alongside the lower operating costs.
