# HOFOR — Fleet Electrification Case Study

**Source file:** `Casehistorier_Fagfolk_templafy.pdf` (compendium; story: "Elbiler et naturligt og miljøbevidst valg")
**Category:** Case study — fleet electrification (utility)
**Original language:** Danish (translated to English below; proper nouns kept in Danish)
**One-line summary:** HOFOR, the Greater Copenhagen utility (Hovedstadsområdets Forsyningsselskab), runs 17 EVs among its ca. 235 passenger/van fleet, adopted pragmatically wherever they can cover the required task.

## Company Profile
- **Company:** HOFOR (Hovedstadsområdets Forsyningsselskab — Greater Copenhagen Utility)
- **Industry/sector:** Utilities
- **Fleet size:** ca. 235 passenger and van vehicles total, of which 17 are electric
- **Location:** Head office in Ørestaden; multiple depot locations
- **Person(s) quoted:** Kenneth Bergstrøm — buyer/administrator of HOFOR's vehicles (indkøber og administrator af HOFORs biler)
- **Website noted in source:** hofor.dk

## Electrification Timeline
- No single "start date" given; adoption described as following "et pragmatisk princip" (a pragmatic principle) — if a task can be handled by an EV, an EV is a real option.
- Fleet as of case writing: 17 EVs among ca. 235 total.
- Examples given:
  - 7 Renault Kangoo Z.E. vans used for smaller operational tasks, alongside a recently acquired Nissan e-NV200.
  - 6 Smart electric vehicles: 4 assigned to building/site managers (byggeledere) who previously drove Volkswagen Touran; these were "overvejende... positivt modtaget" (predominantly well received) after some initial skepticism. The remaining 2 Smart EVs are used as pool cars at the Ørestaden head office.
- HOFOR is enrolled in Hovedstadsregionens elbilpartnerskab and receives Energistyrelsen support on EV purchases; this partnership covers more than 500 EVs across public and private partners.

## Financial Figures
- No specific DKK savings or CO2 tonnage figures are stated in this source.
- Qualitative cost statement: acquisition price for EVs is generally somewhat higher than conventional vehicles, but operating costs are significantly lower; overall total cost of ownership is described as roughly on par with a conventional vehicle ("Samlet set er økonomien i elbiler oftest omtrent den samme som for en konventionel bil").

## Operational Notes
- Core vehicle-selection criteria at HOFOR: must be green, safe, and cheap ("grønt, sikkert og billigt") — green is not pursued "for enhver pris" (at any price).
- Adoption approach is pragmatic and task-driven, not policy-mandated across the whole fleet.
- No V2X or grid-management details mentioned.

## Quotes
- Kenneth Bergstrøm: "Økonomien i elbiler er langt bedre i dag end for bare nogle få år siden... Samlet set er økonomien i elbiler oftest omtrent den samme som for en konventionel bil, hvilket gør elbilen til et oplagt alternativ ikke mindst set i lyset af, at vi opnår miljørigtig kørsel." (The economics of EVs are far better today than just a few years ago... Overall the economics of EVs are usually about the same as a conventional car, which makes the EV an obvious alternative, not least given that we achieve environmentally sound driving.)
- Kenneth Bergstrøm, on future expectations: "Der hvor den grønne transport er, der er HOFOR." (Wherever green transport is, HOFOR is there.)
