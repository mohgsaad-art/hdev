# KLS Grafisk Hus — Fleet Electrification Case Study

**Source file:** `Casehistorier_Fagfolk_templafy.pdf` (compendium; story: "Elbilen er et naturligt valg for Danmarks første CO2-neutrale trykkeri")
**Category:** Case study — fleet electrification (printing industry, CO2-neutral company)
**Original language:** Danish (translated to English below; proper nouns kept in Danish)
**One-line summary:** KLS Grafisk Hus, aiming to be Denmark's greenest printing company and CO2-neutral by end of 2010, cut heating use in half, electricity use by 37%, and switched its company cars to EVs, reporting a 21% cost saving versus its former petrol/diesel fleet.

## Company Profile
- **Company:** KLS Grafisk Hus
- **Industry/sector:** Printing ("grønt tryk" — green print)
- **Fleet:** 3 Renault Fluence (Z.E.), 1 Fiat Qubo E (converted/ombygget), 1 MODEC electric truck (el-lastbil)
- **Annual driving need:** 20,000–35,000 km/year
- **Location:** Not clearly stated in source (company name suggests Danish operations; no city given)
- **Person(s) quoted:** Kasper Larsen — Økonomidirektør (CFO/Finance Director)
- **Related output:** Initiator of "GO" magazine about EVs and plug-in hybrids (go-mag.dk), published quarterly (four times/year)

## Electrification Timeline
- **2007:** Leadership decided to put even greater focus on climate and environment, aiming to become Denmark's greenest printing company and be CO2-neutral by the end of 2010.
- The company mapped three areas responsible for 99% of its direct emissions: heat, electricity, and vehicles ("Varme, elektricitet og biler tegnede sig for 99 pct. af vores direkte udledning").
- Through sustained process-mapping efforts, heating consumption was cut in half and electricity consumption reduced by 37% — using the same machinery.
- Old petrol cars were replaced with EVs, including the executive/director's car — a Fiat Qubo E.
- The company's original ambition was to run only EVs, but this was constrained: after the Better Place bankruptcy and the resulting collapse of the battery-swap technology model, there was (at time of writing) no satisfactory EV alternative for sales staff who drive very high daily mileage. The company has nonetheless committed to buying no more fossil-fuel vehicles going forward, meaning full electrification is "only a matter of time."

## Quotes
- Kasper Larsen: "Vi kortlagde tre områder hvor vi kunne nedbringe vores direkte udledning. Varme, elektricitet og biler tegnede sig for 99 pct. af vores direkte udledning." (We mapped three areas where we could reduce our direct emissions. Heat, electricity and vehicles accounted for 99% of our direct emissions.)
- Kasper Larsen: "Vores målsætning var kun at have elbiler, men efter Better Place konkursen og frafaldet af batteriskifte-teknologien, er der pt. ikke et tilfredsstillende alternativ for vores sælgere, som kører mange kilometer hver dag. Men vi har vedtaget, at vi ikke køber flere fossil biler, så det er kun et spørgsmål om tid, før vi kun har elbiler." (Our goal was to have only EVs, but after the Better Place bankruptcy and the collapse of battery-swap technology, there is currently no satisfactory alternative for our salespeople, who drive many kilometers every day. But we've decided not to buy any more fossil-fuel cars, so it's only a matter of time before we have only EVs.)
- Kasper Larsen: "En elbil er lydløs, men sender et øredøvende signal til omverdenen om, at her er en der har valgt med tanke på klimaet... Når vi kommer ud og fortæller om hvad vi gør på miljø- og klimaområdet, så er det en elbil der holder på parkeringspladsen og ikke en stor benzinsluger. Det giver respekt, at vi gør det vi siger vi gør." (An EV is silent, but sends a deafening signal to the world that here is someone who has chosen with the climate in mind... When we go out and talk about what we do on environment and climate, it's an EV parked outside, not a big petrol guzzler. It commands respect that we do what we say we do.)
- Kasper Larsen: "Jeg har lige haft min Renault Fluence Z.E. til stort service efter 30.000 kilometer og fik en faktura på 758 kr. for serviceeftersynet. Det er billigt." (I just had my Renault Fluence Z.E. in for major service after 30,000 km and got an invoice for 758 DKK for the service check. That's cheap.)
- Kasper Larsen, on founding the GO magazine: "Vi syntes elbilen manglede et talerør, der kunne fortælle og forklare omkring elbilen på elbilens præmisser. Der var særligt tidligere meget negativ omtale, der efter vores opfattelse, ikke var berettiget. Vi ønskede at bidrage til en anden dagsorden og opfattelse af elbilen." (We felt the EV lacked a mouthpiece that could explain the EV on its own terms. There had previously been a lot of negative coverage that, in our view, wasn't warranted. We wanted to contribute to a different agenda and perception of the EV.)

## Future Plans
- Full fleet electrification framed as "only a matter of time," contingent on a satisfactory long-range EV alternative becoming available for high-mileage sales staff.
- Continued publication of GO magazine (go-mag.dk), four issues/year.
