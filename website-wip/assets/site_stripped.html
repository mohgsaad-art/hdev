Inside one depot

01 · On the road

Every route, every stop, every day.

Your real operation goes in first: routes, stops, payload and the hours trucks spend away from the depot.

01 · Overnight

While the city sleeps, the depot charges.

Trucks plug in when the shift ends. EV Scope plans the night so every one is full by morning, under the grid limit.

02 · Battery

Will the battery last the route?

Every route checked against the battery, with charge to spare when the truck is back.

Sized to your hardest route, with margin left at year 8.

03 · Winter

And on the coldest day, at year 8?

Cold air, cabin heating and an aged battery, together. No optimistic averages.

Still enough charge on the coldest morning of the year.

04 · Charging

How big do the chargers need to be?

Just big enough to have every truck full before the morning shift. Parked overnight, even the smallest one is enough.

05 · Payload

How much can it still carry?

Batteries weigh. See the payload left on every route, before you order.

About

Advice before the money is spent.

EV Scope sizes vehicle electrification before anyone signs: the vehicles, the chargers and the grid connection a depot really needs, and what it costs against diesel.

01

Built on EU methods

Vehicle energy follows the methods of EU Regulation 2017/2400, the standard used to certify the energy use of heavy-duty vehicles.

02

Conservative by design

Where the data is uncertain, we choose the option that does not underestimate energy use.

03

Brand-neutral

We don't sell vehicles or chargers. The same numbers work for every supplier.

04

Every source shown

Each input says where it came from, so a board, a bank or a grid company can check it.

Founded in Copenhagen by an energy engineer from DTU, the Technical University of Denmark.
